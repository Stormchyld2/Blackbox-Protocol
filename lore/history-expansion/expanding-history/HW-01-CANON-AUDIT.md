# HW-01 — Historical Canon Audit and Reconciliation

Status: baseline audit and targeted corrections completed; full line-by-line review of every ancillary history remains part of the relevant later phases and HW-18. This register supplements, not replaces, the long-form histories. Scope: repository tree and targeted passages of the public and secret Core histories, F01/F02 and foundation summary, AGENTS, Core canon decisions, 01/01A, 03, 05/05A–05D/05J, 07/07A/08/09, Heritage history, corporate guide, organizations, regions and Core world foundation. Other 02/02A, 03A, 04/04A, 05E–05I, 06/06A were inventoried but not fully reread in this pass. Review before changing their subject matter. No real-world historical fact-check is asserted here.

## Source hierarchy

1. Explicit creator decisions and current adopted canon registers control; F01/F02 establish setting principles. Later dated decisions supersede original open-status statements, not entire historical manuscripts.
2. Core public history controls the public historical narrative; GM Secret History controls classified underlying truth. Neither publication reveals later-era outcomes.
3. Subject-specific dossiers control details within their remit where adopted: 05C founder history; 05D section XIII governance; 05J final milestone; corporate and organization registers; Heritage three-era faction history.
4. Proposed individuals, incidents, sites and campaign outcomes remain working proposals unless developed and explicitly registered under current delegated authority. No blanket promotion of existing proposals.
5. Keep a public event and its classified explanation linked rather than writing incompatible chronologies. A public account can be incomplete, not accidentally contradictory. Keep alternate-history fiction distinguished from actual history.
