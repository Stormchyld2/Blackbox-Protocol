# Collapse Scenario Overlay Framework

Status: creator-approved campaign-design direction, 2026-11-28. Framework name and implementation details are working editorial proposals, not new mechanics. Develop in HW-16 and reconcile with F02, F24, F26, F27, F28 and existing publication scopes before implementation.

## Purpose
The GM can run the same historical world and rules with different independently chosen causes of instability or collapse, years apart, producing distinct campaign histories. No universal collapse cause, mandatory sequence, inevitable threshold or compulsory transition. Core and Signal Lost describe warning signs and potential pressures; Dead Zone supplies a possible starting condition without a definitive origin story.

## Layers
1. Canonical setting: established history, geography, institutions, people, public and restricted knowledge.
2. Existing rules: character, infrastructure, economy, faction, resource, travel, recovery and world progression systems remain authoritative.
3. Scenario overlay: selectable or custom initiating pressures, dates, regional scope, initial conditions, dependencies, uncertainties and optional genre elements.
4. Campaign world state: consequences arising through existing rules, GM adjudication and character actions; recovery, mitigation and divergence remain possible.
5. Campaign canon: recorded outcomes unique to that campaign. Never silently write these back into official canon or another campaign.

## GM preparation template (proposed, nonmechanical)
- Scenario name and starting era/date; affected regions and unaffected regions.
- One or more independent or interacting causes; distinguish known conditions from hypotheses and concealed information.
- Initial infrastructure, institutional, economic and community conditions; relevant existing world-system references.
- Actors, competing objectives, credible responses, warning signs and information accessible to characters.
- Potential escalation, mitigation, stabilization and recovery paths; no predetermined outcome.
- Optional rules or genre modules, explicit dependencies and campaign-specific overrides.
- Record subsequent decisions and world-state changes separately from the reusable scenario definition.

## Illustrative overlays (not canonical events)
A financial and supply-chain crisis; prolonged environmental pressures; international conflict and trade disruption; public-health and institutional stress; infrastructure maintenance and technological dependency; or combinations. Blackbox involvement may be beneficial, harmful, mixed or absent. Specific actors may exploit pressures without becoming universal villains. No operational disaster recipe or new numerical rule is established by these examples.

## Replay and portability
The same baseline can support a new campaign with a new overlay, a variant of a previous overlay, or no collapse pressures at all. Preserve the original campaign record. Reuse scenario definitions separately from outcomes, and permit custom GM-created overlays. Physical worksheets and eventual offline GM Command support should share the same conceptual framework; software must remain optional and GM-controlled. No software implementation is claimed.

## Publication and reconciliation
Use existing publication scopes, particularly Signal Lost, Dead Zone, Transition Handbook and GM Handbook, without introducing a new mandatory purchase. HW-16 reviews earlier narratives as optional example histories rather than universal outcomes. HW-17 checks information firewalls and HW-18 checks dependencies and continuity. Consult existing rules before proposing procedures or numeric thresholds; avoid duplicate simulation engines. The GM decides whether and when a campaign changes eras; dates are metadata, not automatic triggers.
