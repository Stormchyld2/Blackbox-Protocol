# Global History and Worldbuilding Program

## Purpose and scope
A living alternate Earth needs histories of its own, not simply a chronological list of Blackbox operations. Use the existing Core alternate history, GM history, 01A global history, 02A people, 03A conflicts, 04A locations, 05A–J corporations, 06A everyday life and F02 global framework as starting sources. Preserve their full content. The reference games inform depth, navigability and interlocking setting detail, not borrowed lore. The intended result is a reusable source library for the existing twelve-book publication plan, not authorization for new commercial titles.

## Historical layers
- Before 1962: selective historical context reaching as far back as necessary to explain national identities, borders, institutions, trade, inherited conflicts and scientific traditions. Recognizable real history unless an explicit divergence is recorded.
- 1962–1990: Cold War, decolonization, independent scientific and political movements, economic restructuring; Looking Glass is one thread among many.
- 1991–2000: principal divergence, independent regional political choices, emerging institutions, economic and technological shifts.
- 2001–2010: security, diplomacy, development, civil society, trade and institutional reform.
- 2011–2019: new alliances, public controversies, technical standards, social movements and economic consequences.
- 2020–January 2026: emergency responses, rivalries, successes, public memory and contemporary regional conditions.
- 2031 onward: separate Signal Lost and Dead Zone records, default trajectories not compulsory outcomes.

## Editorial standard
Write substantial coherent narrative, not just event lists. Each major invented historical event records date/range, location, participants, prior conditions, cause, outcome, immediate effects, long-term consequences, connections to other regions, public knowledge and campaign relevance. Include genuine successes, failures, ordinary life and independent events. Provide real-world source notes when factual claims require research; clearly distinguish them from invented outcomes. Never attribute fictional crimes to real people or exploit real tragedies as conspiracy explanations. Use existing fictional names only after checking continuity. Preserve the public/GM firewall. Avoid a universal hidden controller, mandatory collapse or compulsory campaign plot.

## Completion and maintenance
Maintain a master chronological index, geographic coverage register, person/institution cross-reference register, and public/classified event links as material grows. These indexes supplement rather than replace narratives. A region is not complete merely because it appears in a table. Track drafted, reconciled, researched and publication-ready status separately. Review the existing source prose and AGENTS before each substantial addition. HW-17 reconciles information boundaries; HW-18 audits dates, geography, causal links, duplication and remaining gaps. No fixed word count or country-by-country completion date is promised; depth and internal consistency govern the work.

## Approved warning-only collapse boundary — 2026-11-28
Supersedes any earlier direction implying a definitive default collapse trajectory. Core and Signal Lost may establish conditions, warning signs, mounting pressures, localized failures, responses and competing interpretations, but must not establish a canonical civilization-wide collapse event, fixed chain of causes, irreversible threshold or mandatory transition. Signal Lost is playable indefinitely; recovery, adaptation and regional divergence remain possible. Blackbox may exacerbate or mitigate pressures, with responsibility attributed to particular actors and decisions. Dead Zone supplies a playable post-collapse starting condition, not a retrospective canonical explanation of how the world reached it. The GM and players establish their own causal history and outcomes. Era dates are setting metadata, not triggers. Existing later-era narratives and faction trajectories must be reviewed in HW-16 and framed as optional scenario histories wherever they imply mandatory outcomes; preserve their useful detail, distinguish scenario assumptions from universal canon, and reconcile publication boundaries in HW-17. Do not treat a later-era starting condition as foreknowledge in Core or Signal Lost. No mechanics changed.
