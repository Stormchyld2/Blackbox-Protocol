## Governing direction
Preserve established canon; record explicit revisions and reconcile affected sources. Develop sustained historical narratives with meaningful alternate outcomes, credible causes and consequences, regional agency and varied human experiences. Halden's humanitarian origins evolve into institutional hubris among some members, contested internally. Blackbox grows institutionally autonomous through distributed authority and dependency; corrupt officials exploit it, but no single actor controls the entire system. Demonstrate genuine benefits as well as abuses. Distinguish publicly knowable history, classified truth, rumor and flexible campaign variables. Treat real tragedies respectfully and label fictional divergences. No inevitable collapse or mandatory metaplot. No new mechanics. Consult AGENTS.md and existing prose before drafting; use source IDs, dates and cross-references. Work one phase at a time; record completed work and remaining checks.

## Global history and worldbuilding depth mandate — 2026-11-28
The creator has expanded the scope to pursue a setting library comparable in ambition and depth to the established reference RPGs (D&D, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu and Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay), without copying their intellectual property or assuming equivalent publication volume. Retain HW-01–18 identifiers. Global history is a parallel subject within each relevant phase, not a subordinate account of Halden's activities. The detailed global work packages in [GLOBAL-HISTORY-PROGRAM.md](GLOBAL-HISTORY-PROGRAM.md) are required deliverables of HW-08/09/11–16, with HW-17/18 auditing the whole corpus. HW-02 remains next. Do not defer all non-Blackbox history until the end or write every global event as a consequence of Blackbox.

## Approved causal and moral boundary — 2026-11-28
Blackbox may contribute to later-era deterioration through its accumulated weaknesses and decisions, never as the sole, necessary or sufficient cause of civilization's collapse. Its accumulated institutional dependency, compromised oversight, conflicting mandates and abuses may amplify independent crises; legitimate capabilities and ethical personnel can also mitigate those crises. Develop multiple interacting political, economic, environmental, technological and social causes with regional variation in HW-07–16. Do not establish an inevitable collapse, a universal villain, a single mastermind or a fixed initiating event. The organization is morally heterogeneous: good-faith service, reform, resistance, negligence, ambition and deliberate wrongdoing coexist. Attribute culpability to specific people, decisions and units rather than collective membership. Players may cooperate with, investigate, oppose, reform or ignore Blackbox. Keep future outcomes out of Core player-facing history. Develop plausible contribution mechanisms and warning signs, not a fixed later-era causal sequence.

## Approved warning-only collapse boundary — 2026-11-28
Supersedes any earlier direction implying a definitive default collapse trajectory. Core and Signal Lost may establish conditions, warning signs, mounting pressures, localized failures, responses and competing interpretations, but must not establish a canonical civilization-wide collapse event, fixed chain of causes, irreversible threshold or mandatory transition. Signal Lost is playable indefinitely; recovery, adaptation and regional divergence remain possible. Blackbox may exacerbate or mitigate pressures, with responsibility attributed to particular actors and decisions. Dead Zone supplies a playable post-collapse starting condition, not a retrospective canonical explanation of how the world reached it. The GM and players establish their own causal history and outcomes. Era dates are setting metadata, not triggers. Existing later-era narratives and faction trajectories must be reviewed in HW-16 and framed as optional scenario histories wherever they imply mandatory outcomes; preserve their useful detail, distinguish scenario assumptions from universal canon, and reconcile publication boundaries in HW-17. Do not treat a later-era starting condition as foreknowledge in Core or Signal Lost. No mechanics changed.

## Reusable scenario overlays — approved direction 2026-11-28
The GM must be able to apply independently chosen or custom causes of deterioration to the same setting and rules, then replay the setting with a different overlay and a separate campaign history. See [Collapse Scenario Overlay Framework](COLLAPSE-SCENARIO-OVERLAY-FRAMEWORK.md). Develop within HW-16 and reconcile in HW-17/18; preserve existing rule authority and manual play. No new mechanics or fixed collapse cause authorized by this design direction.
