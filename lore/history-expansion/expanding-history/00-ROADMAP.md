## Governing direction
Preserve established canon; record explicit revisions and reconcile affected sources. Develop sustained historical narratives with meaningful alternate outcomes, credible causes and consequences, regional agency and varied human experiences. Halden's humanitarian origins evolve into institutional hubris among some members, contested internally. Blackbox grows institutionally autonomous through distributed authority and dependency; corrupt officials exploit it, but no single actor controls the entire system. Demonstrate genuine benefits as well as abuses. Distinguish publicly knowable history, classified truth, rumor and flexible campaign variables. Treat real tragedies respectfully and label fictional divergences. No inevitable collapse or mandatory metaplot. No new mechanics. Consult AGENTS.md and existing prose before drafting; use source IDs, dates and cross-references. Work one phase at a time; record completed work and remaining checks.
