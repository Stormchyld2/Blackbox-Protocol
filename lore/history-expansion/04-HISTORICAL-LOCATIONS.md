# Historical locations and geography
Player-facing gazetteer. Do not invent coordinates or equate fictional regions with real counties.

## Real historical anchors
**Washington, D.C., United States:** National archives, legislative hearings, courts, and public debate over emergency powers.
**Brussels, Belgium:** International policy discussions and institutional coordination; invented Blackbox meetings must be distinguished from actual EU or NATO proceedings.
**Geneva, Switzerland:** Humanitarian and diplomatic institutions; a plausible conference setting, not a claim that real organizations endorsed Blackbox.
**Berlin, Germany:** Cold War division and reunification create layers of physical infrastructure and archival memory.
**Manila, Philippines:** Port commerce, disaster response, journalism, and municipal governance support Philippine-led campaigns.
**Cascadia, United States/Canada:** Broad Pacific Northwest regional context for seismic risk and fictional continuity review.
**Clarksville, Tennessee, United States, and Fort Campbell, Kentucky, United States:** Real civilian and military geography usable as background without attributing fictional wrongdoing to actual personnel.

## Fictional historical sites
**Port Meridian:** Historical designation tied to the 1998 contract dispute. Location and jurisdiction must be established in its own dossier before map placement.
**Harrowgate:** Historical designation for the 2016 surveillance scandal. Not necessarily one town; avoid assigning an unsupported jurisdiction.
**Greybridge:** Designation associated with the 2024 hearings; its exact institutional venue remains to be established.
**Briar Hollow, West Virginia:** Approved fictional starter town in an intentionally unnamed county. Distinct from Calder County and Zero Day.
**Calder County:** Established sample region containing Fenwick and Alder Crossing, with its own emergency infrastructure and Zero Day associations.
**Aldane Coast:** Established fictional coast with Port Aldane and Kestrel Bay.
**Ridgeback Highlands:** Established fictional mountain region with Hollis Gap and its hydroelectric infrastructure.

## Heritage Ranch, Texas
**Placement:** Fictional rural west-central Texas county, broadly between real Abilene and San Angelo, without assigning a real county.
**Founding parcel:** Existing **135-acre cattle ranch**, acquired by Elias Mercer in 2008 for the express purpose of creating a survivalist compound. Livestock supports food independence and finances construction and operations.
**Core facilities:** Ranch entrance with timber HERITAGE RANCH sign; cattle pasture and handling facilities; residences; meeting/worship space; maintenance shops; food storage; agricultural work areas; and security/communications infrastructure added as the project develops. Facility footprints and capacities require a separate map.
**Regional relationships:** Independent farms, local roads, neighboring congregations, county offices, emergency volunteers, and small businesses. Land use must remain physically credible: 135 acres does not support an entire county's population.
**2029 change:** Under Nathan and Rebecca, a new entrance bears THE HERITAGE SETTLEMENT.
**Dead Zone:** Original parcel remains the historical capital; broader territorial control extends over additional holdings and subordinate settlements. It is not a 135-acre county.

## Gazetteer editorial standard
For every future site record specify real/fictional status, era, map reference when established, physical features, governance, economic role, named residents, at least two historical incidents, and publicly discoverable sources.
