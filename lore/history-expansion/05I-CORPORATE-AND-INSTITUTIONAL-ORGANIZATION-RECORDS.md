# Corporate and Institutional Organization Records — 2026 and Three-Era Continuity

**Status:** Working GM reference, companion to the existing ORG-04 Cindrix record in `notes/ORGANIZATIONS.md`. All eight remaining organizations from 05C–05H are covered. These are **companies and a nonprofit institution**, not automatically ideological factions. No additional ORG numbers are assigned pending a canonical identifier review. No new mechanical values, facilities, ownership stakes or inevitable collapse outcomes are established.

**Editorial and rules authority:** `AGENTS.md`, `05C`–`05H`, `notes/ORGANIZATIONS.md` and `notes/DESIGN-NOTES.md`. Existing F23 resource ledgers govern available people, labor, stock and facilities. Clocks, when needed in a specific adventure, use the existing 4/6/8/12 scale. No clocks are active merely because a company is described here.

## Why these records exist

A corporation can make decisions that affect the characters without becoming their enemy. A retailer can protect a community and still have obligations to creditors. A laboratory may refuse to release medicine because its quality staff cannot verify a batch. The GM needs to know who can make the decision, what resources they actually control and who can challenge them. That is the purpose of these organization records.

The histories, boards and executives below follow the established biographies. **Core** describes normal operations in 2026. **Signal Lost** and **Dead Zone** are conditional developments, not fixed endings. In later eras, a surviving store or laboratory is not necessarily still owned by the company whose name is on the sign.

## Halden Institute

**Identity and 2026 base.** Nonprofit research institution; separate from Cindrix and Halden Holdings. **Leadership:** Margaret Halden, trustee chair; Dr. Miriam Calder, executive director. Research Council, Legal and Ethics Committee, trustees; Stewardship, Intervention and Institutional Continuity are internal schools, not subsidiaries.

The institute's public mission grew from Malcolm Halden's concern for resilient infrastructure. Its internal schools disagree about the balance between accountability, intervention and preserving institutional capacity. Their disagreement is not proof that every researcher has knowledge of classified history.

**Core (2026).** Maintain independent research and legitimate oversight. A GM should identify the local decision-maker, the documents or equipment at issue, and the consequences for affected people before initiating an organizational objective.

**Signal Lost (2031–2045).** Preserve research access and review disputed records. Continued operation depends on surviving personnel, communications, materials, customers and legitimate authority. Headquarters may be unreachable; regional managers may disagree about what to preserve.

**Dead Zone (2046 onward).** Protect archives and teach surviving technical knowledge. If the original organization no longer exercises control, track any new cooperative, settlement authority or successor separately. A sign or surviving contract does not establish continuity of governance.

**Resource record.** Consult actual campaign ledgers for research staff, archives, restricted grants, lawful contracts; do not grant national resources to a local site without a credible route, authorization and availability. **Authority boundary:** Trustees cannot commandeer Cindrix or government assets; classification and nonprofit obligations remain distinct.

## Integration and identifier register

The established identifiers remain **ORG-01** (regional infrastructure contractor, optional *CindrixOps* name), **ORG-02** (security/logistics firm), **ORG-03** (civic volunteers) and **ORG-04** (Cindrix Corporation). They are unchanged. The eight organizations in this dossier are recorded **by their existing names**, not assigned speculative ORG-05 onward numbers. This avoids collision with any existing or planned F23 identifier namespace.

The Halden Institute is **not** a Cindrix division. Veydran and Vantrell are separate enterprises, and the five specialist retailers are independent under their respective established ownership arrangements. Do not infer an acquisition, financing relationship or classified collaboration from a shared adventure.

For any organization in a playable scene, the GM records the operating unit, the individual with authority, the current objective, accessible ledger assets, the relationships involved, and the evidence available to characters. If a separate entity acquires its own people, facilities and independent objectives, it may merit a distinct F23 record after identifier reconciliation. Otherwise, a department or branch remains part of its parent organization.

**Completeness boundary:** Together with ORG-04 in `notes/ORGANIZATIONS.md`, this completes the **narrative three-era organization profiles for all nine named corporate/institutional powers** in the current development set: Cindrix, Halden Institute, Veydran, Vantrell, Ridgeline, Sentinel, Ironfield, Harvest & Homestead and CircuitPoint. It does **not** claim that every regional site has numerical staffing, stock or scenario clocks; those require later site-specific play records.
