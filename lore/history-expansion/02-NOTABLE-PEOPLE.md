# People of note: public biographical register
Player-safe. All fictional figures are invented; real historical figures retain their real roles and are not implicated in fictional wrongdoing.

## Historical figures (real)
**John F. Kennedy and Nikita Khrushchev:** Political principals during the 1962 missile crisis. Their actions are studied by fictional continuity scholars, but neither founded Project Looking Glass.
**Mikhail Gorbachev:** His reforms and the dissolution of the Soviet order form essential background to the 1991 divergence.
**Nelson Mandela and F. W. de Klerk:** Central figures in South Africa's democratic transition; their political histories must not be appropriated as Blackbox operations.
**Václav Havel:** Civic opposition and post-communist leadership inform debates about the relationship between state power and civil society.
**Kofi Annan:** United Nations diplomacy and reform are relevant to international coordination debates.
**Wangari Maathai:** Environmental organizing and civic activism exemplify forms of resilience outside government and private security structures.
**Tim Berners-Lee:** The World Wide Web's development anchors the networked society that later continuity systems must serve.
**Angela Merkel and Barack Obama:** Their actual political eras provide context for public policy disputes; do not assign fictional conspiratorial roles.

## Blackbox-era fictional figures
**Nadia Serrano — compliance whistleblower.** Known publicly through the 2016 Harrowgate disclosures. Her professional reputation is divided between praise for accountability and debate over methods of disclosure. GM-only knowledge of confidential records is not reproduced here.
**Elias Ward — investigative journalist.** Builds a career on patient document verification rather than spectacular allegations. His reporting on Harrowgate is publicly influential; he has also issued corrections when sources proved unreliable.
**Martin Kessler — former continuity administrator.** Publicly speaks about institutional memory and legal ambiguity. His guarded testimony attracts speculation but should not be treated as a confession.
**Leena Okafor — compliance analyst.** Advocates clear custodial responsibility, retention schedules, and practical audit trails. Local governments consult her on reform.
**Daniel Rusk — port labor organizer.** Port Meridian dispute makes him a recognizable figure in procurement transparency and worker protection debates. His disagreements with contractors are political, not evidence that all contractors are corrupt.
**Dr. Amara Bell — public-health logistics scholar.** Known for comparative studies of emergency distribution and last-mile delivery. She often challenges simplistic assumptions that centralized stockpiles alone guarantee resilience.
**Rina Mercado — Philippine investigative reporter.** Investigates reconstruction procurement and maritime supply chains, with locally grounded sources and priorities.
**Adewale Bako — infrastructure engineer.** Promotes repairable systems, locally trained technicians, and transparent maintenance contracts.
**Yuki Tanabe — disaster-communications instructor.** Produces widely circulated public training materials on interoperable emergency communication.
**Sofia Valdez — Texas emergency-management specialist.** Cooperates with credible volunteer groups while insisting on inspection, accountability, and lawful boundaries.
**Pastor Miriam Cole — independent minister.** Supports food relief without conditioning it on adherence to Heritage Ranch leadership.
**Caleb Voss — agricultural cooperative organizer.** Defends neighboring farms' autonomy and fair access to roads and markets.

## Heritage family
**Elias Mercer (born 1964):** Former military logistics officer. In 2008 purchases an existing 135-acre cattle ranch to deliberately build Heritage Ranch as a survivalist compound. Cattle provide food self-reliance and revenue to fund the community. Values discipline, faith, voluntary membership, and mutual aid, but fails to build safeguards against concentration of authority.
**Nathan Mercer (born 1985):** Son and security organizer. By Core 2026 his increasingly restrictive approach is a subject of internal disagreement. Later-era details belong in the three-era faction history.
**Rebecca Mercer (born 1989):** Daughter and educator/administrator. By Core 2026 she argues for stronger doctrinal and membership obligations; future conduct is not yet public history.

## Editorial biographical requirements
Before final publication each invented figure should receive verified birth year or an explicitly unspecified date, home region, career milestones, affiliations, public reputation, relationships, and at least two playable contacts or records. Do not manufacture biographies for real people beyond their historical record.
