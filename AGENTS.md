# Development instructions

Consult the foundation summary, notes/DESIGN-NOTES.md (combined design notes and decision register, including the Outstanding work list), existing rules and publication files before changes. Preserve approved decisions; do not duplicate mechanics or silently introduce unapproved rules. User retains final authority; detailed recommendations and audit resolutions may be developed under explicit delegation. Existing approved mechanics remain unless a documented correction is needed. Numerical defaults require playtesting; unresolved audit items are not completed rules. Keep tabletop independent, software offline-first, and public repository free of restricted third-party content. Audit reused code for compatibility, security and licensing. Document dependencies, probability, examples, tests and migrations. Maintain one authoritative source per mechanic. See docs/foundation/F01-GAME-IDENTITY.md for the twenty guardrails.
