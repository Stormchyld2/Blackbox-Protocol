# Blackbox System Reference Document — scope and release specification

Owner-approved licensing direction; pre-release specification, not a license grant or completed SRD.

Designated SRD material will be released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), proposed for final legal review. License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode . Only material explicitly identified in the released SRD is covered. CC BY permits commercial reuse, adaptation and redistribution of that material with attribution; it is irrevocable for compliant recipients. Do not include proprietary material that the owner wishes to control.

## Inclusion schedule
- Core D100 resolution, degrees, difficulty, opposed checks and assistance.
- Seven attributes, derived statistics, general skill names and creation/advancement procedures.
- Generic competency framework and original neutral examples, not the complete proprietary occupation or competency catalogues.
- Generic time, action, injury, stress, inventory, resource and project interfaces.
- Homebrew record templates and compatibility identifiers.

## Exclusion schedule
Official setting lore, named characters and factions, adventures including Zero Day, full catalogues, original artwork, logos, maps, trade dress, software source and proprietary manuscript expression outside the designated SRD. Third-party material excluded unless independently cleared. Abstract mechanics and other unprotectable material remain subject to applicable law.

## Release procedure
Extract independently authored rules from current approved sources, reconcile F08/F09 acceptance, assign stable rule IDs, remove proprietary examples, review rights and cross-references, pin version, publish separate SRD with exact attribution and license notice. Do not apply CC BY to repository root or entire books. Maintain manifest of included sections and versions. No actual SRD release authorized by this draft.
