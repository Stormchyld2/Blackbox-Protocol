# Blackbox Compatibility and Trademark Policy — pre-release draft

Proposed permission for truthful plain-text compatibility statements, e.g. "Compatible with Blackbox Protocol", on independently created products. Identify third-party creator prominently. Suggested disclaimer: "An independent third-party product. Not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Blackbox Protocol or its rights holder."

No official logos, emblems, distinctive trade dress, official product designation or confusingly similar branding without separate written permission. No claim of certification unless actually granted. Compatibility statement should identify applicable rules/SRD version and disclose material deviations. Do not imply official setting canon.

Nothing here restricts lawful nominative or descriptive use or other statutory rights. Trademark ownership, clearance, registration status and territorial scope require verification; do not use registered trademark symbol absent registration. Separate optional compatibility badge may be designed and licensed later, not granted now. Effective date, rights holder identity and legal review pending.
## Commercial compatibility terms
Upon effective publication, permission for truthful plain-text compatibility statements applies equally to paid and free products without royalty or application. Use the creator's own title and identity prominently; compatibility wording subordinate and not confusingly similar to official branding. No official logo, badge, domain/account impersonation or claim of sponsorship. Correct misleading statements upon notice; any withdrawal of separate trademark permission does not affect CC BY rights or lawful descriptive use. No registration claim. Effective date and verified rights holder/contact required before activation.
