# Blackbox Community Content Policy — pre-release draft

## Permission model
After publication of the designated SRD, creators may make free or paid compatible adventures, occupations, competencies, equipment, settings, optional rules and tools using their original work and licensed SRD material under its applicable license. No royalty or prior approval proposed for SRD-only compatible creations. Creators retain ownership of their original contributions; no assignment to Blackbox is required.

## Boundaries
This policy does not grant permission to reproduce or distribute official books, proprietary setting expression, adventures, full catalogues, art, logos or software outside the SRD. Use of those protected materials requires separate written permission unless otherwise legally permitted. No implication of endorsement. Third-party rights remain the creator's responsibility. Do not present a third-party product as an official release.

## Attribution
Identify the released SRD version, author/rightsholder as specified in its notice, source URL, CC BY 4.0 link and changes where required. Do not impose additional restrictions on recipients' exercise of CC BY rights. Compatibility branding is separately governed; it does not narrow the SRD license. No mandatory marketplace or exclusive distribution channel proposed.

## Additional permissions
Setting-specific licensed products, official art, translations of proprietary books and software redistribution require separate written agreements where protected rights are implicated. Contact route to be supplied by owner. Policy changes prospective; cannot revoke existing compliant CC BY rights. This draft does not itself grant rights; effective date and legal review pending.
