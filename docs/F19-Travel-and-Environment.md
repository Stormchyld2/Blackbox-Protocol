# F19 Travel and Environment

Canonical DESIGN-NOTES F19; existing F04–F14 mechanics remain authoritative. Delegated working foundation, not final publication or human-tested rules.

## Route model
Map actual geography separately from character knowledge. Record route segments, distance, mode, terrain, access, weather, declared speed/duration, supplies, load and hazard events. F11 clock advances actual elapsed time, including stops and detours; no per-mile mandatory roll. F06 encounter movement conversion is not sustained travel speed. F17 load reference and F20 resources remain authoritative.
## Provisional gameplay procedure
GM declares segment speed and exertion budget before departure using fictional conditions and available equipment. Duration=distance/speed for feasible positive speed, plus explicit stops; zero speed means blocked, not infinite elapsed time. Round once to integer seconds upward. Optional delegated abstract load adjustment for foot travel: burdened speed ×0.75, heavily burdened ×0.5, normal ×1; no second modifier for same burden. No real-world travel recommendation. Exertion cost declared per segment in stamina units; clamp at0, stop strenuous activity at exhaustion, no automatic HP conversion. Hazards use existing core checks and F12/F13 effects; exposure is distinct from exertion and stress. No automatic damage merely from era or terrain label.
## Modes and records
Standard narrative blocks with declared duration and supply costs; Advanced segment routes, load and weather; Simulation detailed maintenance and schedules. Interrupt at meaningful hazard/deadline, retain completed distance, recalculate remaining route. Example fictional 12 distance units at 4 units/hour takes3h, plus1h detour=4h. Route sheet, map annotation, travel log and hazard IDs. Actual speed/exertion tables require F36 calibration; no fixed real-world performance data.

## Cross-foundation reconciliation and verification (2026-11-26)

**Reference verification: conditionally passed.** `tests/f19-travel-reference.test.mjs` covers eight travel-time, load, interruption and exertion cases; `tests/f19-environment-resources-reference.test.mjs` covers eight resource and environmental-event cases. Their test functions have been executed in an equivalent JavaScript harness with 16/16 passing. The full repository Node test-runner, production implementation, scenario balance calibration and human tabletop playtests remain open; these fixtures are not evidence of final playtest certification.

**Authority boundaries:** F11 advances elapsed campaign time, including stops and detours. F17 determines carried mass and provisional load band; F19 applies its optional foot-travel speed factor once, not to vehicle travel and not twice for the same burden. F20 owns supply lots, units, reservations and actual consumption: a completed travel segment may charge only its declared resource cost, and an interrupted or blocked future segment may not charge its supplies in advance. Abstract daily food/water demand is a separate F20 interval and must not be deducted a second time as a travel expense for the same units. F13 owns stamina and harm; F19 exertion clamps stamina at zero but does not convert to HP damage. Exposure, psychological stress and hazard injury require their applicable governing procedures, not automatic weather-label damage. Record unique segment and hazard IDs to prevent replay; validate hazard effect authority before recording an event as processed.

**Outstanding checks:** GM-declared terrain/weather speed and exertion balance, mixed-mode travel and transfers, route-knowledge versus map-truth scenarios, shortage interruptions, shared supplies and concurrent travellers, F11 calendar boundaries, F20 daily ration overlap, and actual F36 human playtesting. No new movement rates, environmental damage values or mandatory per-distance checks are approved by this reconciliation. F19 may advance to F20 at the foundation-reference stage while these gates remain explicit.
