# F19 Travel and Environment

Canonical DESIGN-NOTES F19; existing F04–F14 mechanics remain authoritative. Delegated working foundation, not final publication or human-tested rules.

## Route model
Map actual geography separately from character knowledge. Record route segments, distance, mode, terrain, access, weather, declared speed/duration, supplies, load and hazard events. F11 clock advances actual elapsed time, including stops and detours; no per-mile mandatory roll. F06 encounter movement conversion is not sustained travel speed. F17 load reference and F20 resources remain authoritative.
## Provisional gameplay procedure
GM declares segment speed and exertion budget before departure using fictional conditions and available equipment. Duration=distance/speed for feasible positive speed, plus explicit stops; zero speed means blocked, not infinite elapsed time. Round once to integer seconds upward. Optional delegated abstract load adjustment for foot travel: burdened speed ×0.75, heavily burdened ×0.5, normal ×1; no second modifier for same burden. No real-world travel recommendation. Exertion cost declared per segment in stamina units; clamp at0, stop strenuous activity at exhaustion, no automatic HP conversion. Hazards use existing core checks and F12/F13 effects; exposure is distinct from exertion and stress. No automatic damage merely from era or terrain label.
## Modes and records
Standard narrative blocks with declared duration and supply costs; Advanced segment routes, load and weather; Simulation detailed maintenance and schedules. Interrupt at meaningful hazard/deadline, retain completed distance, recalculate remaining route. Example fictional 12 distance units at 4 units/hour takes3h, plus1h detour=4h. Route sheet, map annotation, travel log and hazard IDs. Actual speed/exertion tables require F36 calibration; no fixed real-world performance data.
