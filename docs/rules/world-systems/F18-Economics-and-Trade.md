# F18 Economics and Trade

Canonical DESIGN-NOTES F18; existing F04–F14 mechanics remain authoritative. Delegated working foundation, not final publication or human-tested rules.

## Markets and value
Market ID, location, date, accepted currencies/units, stock, demand, price quote, validity, access and counterparties. Blackbox Protocol era uses actual local currency (2026 USD for the US baseline); Signal Lost permits mixed currency and exchange where accepted; Dead Zone defaults to local accounting/barter. There is no universal exchange rate. Prices are local explicit quotes, not inferred from era. Standard GM affordability and agreed exchange; Advanced quantities and regional quotes; Simulation production/labor/transport and trends. Currency ownership persists when acceptance disappears.
## Transactions
Quote quantity×unit price in integer smallest currency units; explicit fees and rounding at quote stage. Validate funds, stock, acceptance, access and consent before atomic exchange; reject insufficient balances without mutation. Barter records both item legs and agreed quantities, no forced equal nominal value. Credit records principal, currency/unit, parties, due date, terms and repayment; no automatic interest absent agreement. Employment records compensation, work interval and obligations; F11 time, F17 stock. No duplicated income or sale of reserved stock.
## Starting funds and standard monthly accounting (simplified; owner-approved 2026-11-26)
The contemporary 2026 US baseline grants **$2,500 total accessible starting funds per character**. The player divides that amount between physical cash and bank deposits; neither portion is an additional grant. Extra savings, vehicles, property, debts and occupational equipment require established background and GM approval. Occupation does not increase starting funds.

For characters maintaining ordinary financial obligations, resolve finances **once per in-game month**: take-home occupational pay or other established income, minus one lifestyle expense and significant debt payments. Use approximate realistic 2026 US net occupational pay, with sensible adjustments for experience or location. Lifestyle choices are **Poverty, Modest, Comfortable, Affluent and Wealthy**; each has one GM-established monthly reference cost. Routine purchases are covered by that expense; track exceptional purchases separately. Income ends or changes when its source does. Keep character-sheet fields compact: physical cash, bank balance, net monthly income, lifestyle expense, debt payments and remainder. Salary and lifestyle dollar tables require separate calibration; no unapproved figures are implied here.

GM adjustments for unusually costly locations, other campaign years, shared households, dependents or financial hardship are **guidance, not mandatory calculations**. Advanced/Simulation may optionally track individual bills, interest, taxes and business activity. Do not create a separate hardship mechanic: apply existing survival, health and psychological consequences only when actual circumstances warrant them. In Signal Lost and Dead Zone, use locally accepted currency, market scarcity, barter, labor and surviving assets as infrastructure changes; there is no automatic replacement $2,500 grant. The existing optional Means 0–6 system remains an **alternative** to cash tracking, never simultaneous for the same character.

Reference fixture only: 3 units at 400 cents each cost 1,200 cents; 2,000 cents becomes 800 cents. Existing fictional-product catalogue price tiers are not researched retail prices. Signal Lost scarcity guidance remains plentiful ×1, short ×2–5, scarce ×5–20, unavailable = no sale, subject to GM-established local markets. F36 economic calibration remains pending.
