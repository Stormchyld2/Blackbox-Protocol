## Framework

Occupations represent documented experience, not classes or permanent skill locks. Preserve the approved fourteen families: Investigator, Law Enforcement, Military, Medical, Scientist, Technology Specialist, Engineer, Tradesperson, Outdoorsman, Agriculturalist, Emergency Responder, Criminal, Academic & Researcher, Civilian. Setting recommendations are not eligibility locks. Current role and prior background are separate. Support custom occupations and status-only characters through the same allocation rules, with no invented professional credentials.

Primary general allocations BP100/SL80/DZ60; zero/one/two secondary backgrounds at20 each. Combined occupational contribution max30 per skill, redistribute excess in relevant five-point increments. Personal general allocation and attribute rules remain F07. Competency grants represent justified training; merge canonical IDs by maximum level, validate supporting prerequisites and equivalent training, recalculate CDR. No automatic professional authority, property, institutional access, or cumulative advantage stacking. Personal competency purchases follow CCDD-12/19/20/22. Career change after creation uses development projects and actual history, never another starting package.

Advantages must be grounded, contextual and verified, not guaranteed success. Resources distinguish owned, borrowed, institutional, consumable and unavailable; campaign conditions determine actual access. Adaptation is task-specific and evidenced, not automatic era-wide skill loss. Individual profile authoring remains in Section 6.5; do not overwrite parallel editorial work.
