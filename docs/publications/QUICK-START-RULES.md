# Quick-Start Rules

Status: Approved publication concept; development outline, not a finished rulebook.

## Purpose and scope

Condensed, directly compatible introduction to the core rules; basic creation, dice, investigation, combat, conditions, advancement, pregenerated characters and quick references.

## Shared requirements

- Tabletop first; offline digital enhancement later.
- Follow approved foundation decisions and development guardrails.
- Preserve GM authority, homebrew flexibility and shared rules compatibility.
- Do not establish unapproved mechanics or duplicate authoritative rules.

## Development status

Detailed chapter structure, mechanics, artwork, research, examples and playtesting remain pending.

## Established foundation requirements

Same core mechanics and transferable characters; teach through play, pregen and custom options, manual or digital dice, essential clues have multiple routes. No final dice thresholds or character creation tables yet.

See [Foundation Summary](../foundation/FOUNDATION-SUMMARY.md) and [Design Notes and Decision Register](../../notes/DESIGN-NOTES.md). Initial publication scope is locked to twelve; specialized expansions deferred. These requirements are design inputs, not finished manuscript rules.
