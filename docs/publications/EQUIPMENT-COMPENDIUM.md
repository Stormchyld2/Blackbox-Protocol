# Equipment Compendium

**Status:** Approved publication concept; **working 145-item game-reference catalogue and Core equipment draft exist**, but the Equipment Compendium is not a finished, playtest-certified manuscript.

## Purpose and scope

Equipment, tools, vehicles and resources across the Blackbox Protocol, Signal Lost and Dead Zone eras. The Core Rulebook owns universal equipment and inventory procedures under F17; this publication expands the catalogue and references those rules without inventing a competing inventory system.

## Editorial and mechanical boundaries

Record each item by stable EQ ID, unit and quantity, mass in pounds/ounces, relevant compatibility, availability by era, condition and maintenance needs. The F17 carrying-load *reference* is derived in kilograms and converted to pounds when comparing against item masses; it is not a second item-unit system. The three-era availability codes are gameplay abstractions, not predictions of real-world stock or future prices.

Distinguish ownership, custody, location and authorized access. Employer-issued or shared stock is not automatically personal property; transfers, reservations and consumption must reconcile to one authoritative ledger. Empty containers, unloaded weapons and multi-item bundles do not generate free contents, ammunition or duplicate items. Standard, Advanced and Simulation use the existing F17 configuration rather than new Compendium-specific rules.

Use fictional, generic equipment labels for the game-facing catalogue. Any external research belongs in a separate sourced research layer; game-reference estimates must not be described as verified retail prices.
