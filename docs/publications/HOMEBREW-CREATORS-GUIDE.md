# Homebrew Creator's Guide

Status: Approved publication concept; development outline, not a finished rulebook.

## Purpose and scope

Custom occupations, skills, NPCs, monsters, scenarios, settings, mechanics and content packages; preserve official sources.

## Shared requirements

- Tabletop first; offline digital enhancement later.
- Follow approved foundation decisions and development guardrails.
- Preserve GM authority, homebrew flexibility and shared rules compatibility.
- Do not establish unapproved mechanics or duplicate authoritative rules.

## Development status

Detailed chapter structure, mechanics, artwork, research, examples and playtesting remain pending.

## Established foundation requirements

Official core/settings/supplements/homebrew/campaign overrides; preserve originals, explicit dependencies and conflicts, versioned packages, no coding required. Custom occupations, skills, NPCs, monsters, settings, adventures and rules. Licensing pending.

See [Foundation Summary](../foundation/FOUNDATION-SUMMARY.md) and [Decision Register](../../notes/DECISION-REGISTER.md). Initial publication scope is locked to twelve; specialized expansions deferred. These requirements are design inputs, not finished manuscript rules.
