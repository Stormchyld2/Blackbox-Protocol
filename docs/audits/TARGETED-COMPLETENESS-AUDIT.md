## Classification and decision rules
- **COMPLETE:** procedural inputs, decisions, resolution and consequences specified in reviewed source.
- **UNDERDEVELOPED:** procedure exists but leaves recurring decisions without bounded guidance.
- **MISSING:** reviewed sources show no usable procedure for a required task; confirm against full canonical Design Notes before canonizing a new rule.
- **DOCUMENTATION ONLY:** executable procedure exists; newcomer-facing sequence, example or quick reference is absent.
- **OTHER BOOK / OPTIONAL:** deliberately owned elsewhere, not a Core mechanical defect.
- **UNVERIFIED:** cannot establish absence/completeness without deeper canonical or catalogue inspection.
Severity: **BLOCKER** stops ordinary core play; **MAJOR** likely recurring inconsistent GM decisions; **MINOR** rare/clarity; **OPTIONAL** not core. All findings below are **audit issues**, not new mechanics.

## Confirmed cross-system observations
1. **No demonstrated missing universal dice engine, combat damage rule, advancement rule or injury rule** in the reviewed foundation files. Do not redesign them by analogy to D&D or Pathfinder.
2. The most consistent deficit is **GM operational support**, not foundational arithmetic: first-session onboarding (CA-19), difficulty adjudication examples (CA-03), rapid NPC creation (CA-05), encounter preparation (CA-11), investigation rescue (CA-08), faction/world operation (CA-15).
3. The F19 text expressly calls **travel speed/exertion tables** subject to calibration; F20 labels its abstract consumption defaults provisional. Check later Design Notes approvals before marking a live numeric gap.
4. The F17 item catalogue and F21/F22 recipes/projects are **content and calibration** concerns, not automatically missing core rules. Respect Equipment Compendium and Survival Handbook ownership.
5. F26 specifies a Zero Day teaching sequence, **not** a complete playable adventure manuscript. The adventure content gap cannot be closed by passing reference fixtures.
6. F29 requires a full rule-ID/edition, examples, printable trackers and publication review contract. That is a **publication-readiness gate**, not proof the underlying mechanics are absent.

## Comparison against mature RPG completeness
**D&D 5e/2024:** Functionally requires creation, adjudication, combat, advancement and usable GM encounter prep. Blackbox has documented core equivalents but lacks equivalent packaged GM examples/tools. No obligation to reproduce spells/classes/CR.  
**Pathfinder 2e:** Strong benchmark for explicit action procedures, conditions, character option navigation and GM encounter guidance. Blackbox has defined six-second action economy and conditions but lacks a similarly usable GM prep path; no obligation to adopt Pathfinder's tactical or encounter math.  
**Call of Cthulhu 7e:** Strong benchmark for percentile resolution, investigative pacing, keeper guidance and runnable scenarios. Blackbox has D100 and redundant clue-route principles; its practical GM case facilitation and completed starter adventure remain behind. No obligation to adopt sanity mechanics.

**Overall disposition:** **No proven core-play mechanical blocker in the reviewed files; six high-value GM support gaps; some unresolved calibration/source checks; manuscript and starter adventure unfinished.** This is an **initial targeted foundation audit**, not certification that every canonical Design Notes edge case or all catalogue interactions have been checked.
