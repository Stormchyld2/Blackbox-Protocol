# F12 — Combat

Fictional tabletop conflict reference. Canonical DESIGN-NOTES Section4.1–4.2, R01–R06, F06 and F11. No real-world weapon performance or tactical instruction. No new numeric rules.

## Encounter procedure
Establish fictional stakes, participants, awareness, positions, feasible actions and consequences. Use F11 six-second rounds, derived initiative, one main, movement and reaction; main may convert to movement. No banked actions or free surprise round. End when stakes resolve, disengagement occurs or meaningful opposition ends. Player agency remains; surrender and negotiation are legitimate outcomes.

## Resolution
One D100 using existing relevant skill, contextual attribute adjustment, highest applicable competency bonus, difficulty and distinct modifiers. Routine/impossible determined before rolling; possible uncertain target clamp1–99. Natural01 critical success, 100 critical failure. Exceptional <=floor(T/5), strong <=floor(T/2), standard <=T, otherwise failure. Aware eligible defender may declare reaction before dice: evasion at floor(DEX/2) or applicable Close Combat defense (half-DEX evasion approved 2026-11-25, provisional pending playtests). Compare category then signed margin; attacker must succeed and strictly win, tie or both fail no hit. No reaction available means ordinary check. No extra attacks from retreat or numeric multiple-opponent bonus. Abstract engaged/near/far/out-of-reach bands; equipment defines feasibility; complete obstruction prevents direct attack. Partial/substantial cover -10/-20 only when not already included in difficulty. No double-counted cover, armor or impairment.

## Harm and protection
On successful harmful action one abstract severity die D4/D6/D8/D10 (low/moderate/high/extreme); degree bonus standard0,strong1,exceptional2,critical3. Applicable armor0–3 subtracts once; loss=max(0,die+bonus−armor). No minimum penetrating damage, extra die or armor roll. Environmental harm same abstract profile without degree bonus unless specified. F13 owns HP clamp, injury classification and care. Feasible genuinely noninjurious effects use stamina rather than HP; declaration alone cannot make harmful acts harmless. Zero stamina blocks strenuous activity, no automatic HP conversion. Advanced fictional functional objective may use existing -20 modifier and temporary condition instead of additional harm; no anatomical tables. Simulation tracks equipment state only on explicit wear events. F17 owns equipment assignments.

## Modes and records
Standard abstract positions and most consequential condition; Advanced distance and conditions; Simulation equipment and resource records, same checks. Encounter sheet: participant ID, initiative, awareness, position, action usage, declared intent, target, modifiers, roll/category, opposition, abstract harm, protection, pool change, condition reference, elapsed time. GM adjudicates incompatible simultaneous actions. No automatic morale roll; F14/F16 govern pressure and social consequences.
