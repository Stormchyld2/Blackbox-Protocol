# F10 — Advancement and Career Evolution

Foundation working reference. DESIGN-NOTES.md controls mechanics; F07–F09 and CCDD-12/19/20/22 control creation and competencies. No new character levels or numeric defaults.

## 1. General skill advancement
One mark per skill per meaningful scenario or distinct completed 40 active hours of relevant nonroutine practice. Stakes or feedback required; no roll farming. Partial hours carry forward. Resolve marks chronologically: D100 strictly above current underlying skill grants 1D4 capped99; failure consumes mark; at99 no check needed. Advancement roll is not task resolution: 100 qualifies for advancement below99. No competency bonus or contextual modifier on advancement. Training credit assigned once to primary route; distinct incidental activity may qualify separately.

## 2. Attributes
At least30 in-world days of sustained relevant development, documented evidence and GM approval; one eligible attribute per monthly checkpoint. D100 strictly above underlying grants +1 capped100. No automatic monthly award, no automatic healing; recalculate dependent statistics per F06. Optional Advanced/Simulation disuse review after six months without meaningful use: contextual unfamiliarity first, optional -5 contextual penalty, recover through demonstrated practice, no automatic underlying loss.

## 3. Competency advancement
Use F09 sequential gates, supporting prerequisites, CDR thresholds, access, resources, assessment, shared slots and hours. Award incremental CDR only on approved completion; no character XP or level. Career retraining is a project classification within shared capacity, not another reward. Preserve earned learning when access lapses.

## 4. Adaptation
Track context-specific deficit magnitude separately from underlying skill. Do not stack era defaults; prior relevant history can reduce or waive deficit. Each distinct accepted milestone removes min(5,outstanding deficit). Familiarization, guided practice, independent application and advanced adaptation are evidence types, not automatic weekly awards. Normally at least8 active hours plus demonstration, or GM-approved equivalent meaningful field experience. No duplicated evidence. Leadership75 with DZ deficit10 resolves at65 before other modifiers; two distinct milestones restore75. Missing facilities remain feasibility constraints. Declare new learning objectives on transition; no fresh creation package or budget.

## 5. Shared development ledger
Project fields: ID, character, type, target/context, prior state, requirements, route, instructor/resources, planned/credited active hours, evidence IDs, assessment, state, timestamps, approval, outcome transaction ID. States planned/locked/ready/active/paused/assessment pending/completed. Only active and assessment pending consume slots; pause preserves progress. Validate time availability, allocate each entry once, deduplicate evidence and outcome transactions. Completion atomically applies one outcome; failed assessment retains eligible hours and requires corrective practice. Failed general advancement consumes mark. Standard summarizes, Advanced itemizes, Simulation schedules; outcomes identical. Transition reevaluates access/capacity without erasing earned progress.

## 6. Career evolution
Retain original occupation and chronological roles. New role requires actual demonstrated capability, training or equivalent qualification, appointment where applicable, and access; no automatic promotion or free package. Record scope, issuer/equivalent, restrictions and expiry separately from competencies. Leadership, relationships, reputation and community achievements are narrative/world outcomes, not automatic skill or CDR awards. Use F16/F23/F24 for social and world mechanics when authored. Retirement/succession: archive character history, transfer only legitimately transferable assets/duties subject to campaign authority; successor uses F07 or documented history, never inherits percentages/CDR. This procedural treatment is a delegated interpretation of F10 goals, not a new reward system.

## 7. Worked examples
160 distinct eligible general practice hours generate four marks; 160 idle hours generate none. At skill75, roll76 then improvement4 gives79; next roll78 fails and consumes mark. At98, successful advancement caps99. Attribute80 with approved monthly evidence and roll81 becomes81; repeated same month rejected. A BP-origin character entering DZ retains CDR and acquired levels; current-era capacity changes, no DZ personal creation budget. A paused project retains hours but releases slot. A completed adaptation milestone cannot also award a competency level using the same activity.
