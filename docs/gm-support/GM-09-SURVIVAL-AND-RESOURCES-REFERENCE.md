## Daily baseline (approved)
| Resource | Default demand |
|---|---|
| Food | **1 ration unit per character per day** |
| Water | **1 ration unit per character per day** |

GM declares altered demand before the interval when fictional conditions justify it (e.g. heat/exertion or cold/inadequate shelter). A unit is an **abstract game ration**, not a physical medical standard. Do not infer that a retail package equals one ration unit.

## Provisional deprivation worksheet — NOT APPROVED AS FINAL
Design Notes §7.3 proposes fractional food and water shortage counters, unrecovered shortage stamina, and the thresholds below. **Do not silently enforce this draft in a published game or claim its numbers are locked.** It requires owner confirmation and playtest.

| Draft condition | Water shortage counter | Food shortage counter | Draft effect |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Deprived | 2+ | 4+ | −10 to physical tasks; no HP recovery |
| Severely deprived | 3+ | 7+ | −20 physical/sustained concentration; no HP recovery or sleep-based stamina recovery |
| Critical deprivation | 5+ | 21+ | −30 or infeasible; life-threatening condition only when GM establishes and records reason |

**Draft counter method:** Daily add missing fraction of declared demand; fully met day subtracts 0.25 from that category, minimum zero. A fully unmet category costs 5 stamina; partial shortage costs ceil(5 × missing fraction). Shortage stamina remains unrecovered until both counters clear; no double charge for the same day. This is a **playtest proposal**, not a physiology model. Deprivation penalties use the highest applicable injury-type penalty once; stress remains separate. The first severe deprivation event in an episode is a moderate pressure event under §4.4 (draft).

**Other supply use (draft §7.3):** Standard ammunition uses per encounter when fired; medical use per stabilization/daily care; repair use per project. Keep these provisional pending confirmation.

**GM boundary:** Use existing injury, stress and environmental harm rules where applicable. No independent dehydration death clock or real-world treatment claims.
