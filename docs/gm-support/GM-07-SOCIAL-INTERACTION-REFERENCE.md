## Adjudicate social scenes
| GM question | Rule-facing answer |
|---|---|
| What does NPC want? | Identify goals, constraints, obligations and relationship |
| What is player requesting? | State intent, leverage, plausible result and stakes |
| Would NPC plainly agree? | Resolve without roll |
| Is concession impossible? | No roll; explain fictional barrier |
| Is outcome uncertain? | Use appropriate Persuasion, Intimidation, Deception, Leadership or justified attribute |
| Is NPC actively opposing? | Apply existing opposed D100 checks, R01–R03 |
| Does success compel belief or reveal truth? | **No**; success achieves feasible declared result only |
| Can player repeat same failed pitch? | Not without changed circumstances, new leverage or meaningful cost |
| Who controls player beliefs and choices? | **The player** |

## Keep these distinct
| Record | Key questions |
|---|---|
| Relationship/trust | Between which parties? What has been demonstrated? |
| Reputation | Which audience, place and topic? Source/date? Rumor or verified deed? |
| Authority | Who granted it, for what scope, when valid, with what restrictions? |
| Obligation | Parties, terms, due date and fulfilled/disputed status? |
| Affiliation | Membership or alignment; not automatic universal trust |

**Example (F16):** A former official may retain expertise and local goodwill after collapse but does not automatically retain lawful authority in a different settlement.
