## Starting funds (contemporary US baseline, 2026)
| Entry | Rule |
|---|---|
| Total starting accessible funds | **$2,500** |
| Physical cash + bank deposits | Must sum to **$2,500**; not separate grants |
| Extra assets/debts | Require documented background and GM approval |
| Occupational equipment | Separate; never convert kit to free money |
| SL / DZ transition | No new $2,500 grant; retain only actual accessible assets |

**Example:** $400 physical cash + $2,100 bank = **$2,500 total**.

## Scarcity and access (F18)
| Market scarcity | Reference price guidance | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Plentiful | ×1 | Available if market has stock and access |
| Short | ×2–5 | Market-specific |
| Scarce | ×5–20 | Market-specific |
| Unavailable | No sale | Cannot buy here |

Multipliers are **GM market guidance**, not a global era-wide price change. Confirm stock, accepted tender, access and agreement before transactions. In Signal Lost, currency works only where accepted. In Dead Zone, barter, labor and local accounting are defaults; no universal exchange rate. Record both sides of barter.

## Optional Means alternative (never combine with cash accounting)
| Means tier | Label | Reference 2026 USD price tier |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Destitute / trivial | Under $10 |
| 1 | Struggling / cheap | $10–50 |
| 2 | Getting by / moderate | $50–250 |
| 3 | Comfortable / expensive | $250–1,000 |
| 4 | Prosperous / major | $1,000–5,000 |
| 5 | Wealthy / asset | $5,000–50,000 |
| 6 | Rich / institutional | Over $50,000 |

Below Means: routine; at Means: roughly once monthly without strain; above Means: needs arrangement, savings, credit or sale. Means changes with documented events, not minor purchases. Choose **Means or currency**, never both for the same character.
