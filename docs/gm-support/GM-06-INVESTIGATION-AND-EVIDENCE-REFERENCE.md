# GM-06 — Investigation and Evidence GM Tables
**Sources:** `docs/F15-Investigation-and-Evidence.md`; `notes/DESIGN-NOTES.md` F15, R01–R03; F26. **Status:** Canonical-source-derived aid, no new resolution rules.

## Run an investigation
| GM decision | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Establish truth | Record what happened, who was involved, timeline, motive and locations **privately** |
| Establish essential conclusion | Provide at least **two independent discovery routes**; prefer three |
| Obvious accessible evidence | Reveal **without a roll** |
| Uncertain details | Select Search (observation), Research (records), Forensics (interpretation), or PER (immediate cues) |
| Failed check | Apply declared time/detail/reliability consequence; **do not erase obvious evidence** |
| Competing accounts | Record separately; no automatic truth detection |
| Verification | Corroboration or appropriate method; custody gap creates uncertainty, not automatic falsity |
| Player inference | Present observations and uncertainty; players decide what they believe |
| Puzzle obstacle | Supply equivalent accessible route rather than requiring specialized player knowledge |
