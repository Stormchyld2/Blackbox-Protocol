# F30 Licensing and Intellectual Property

Canonical DESIGN-NOTES F30; delegated working foundation. Existing approved decisions preserved.

## Rights policy
Original expression, terminology, mythology, adventures and art. Inspiration is not authorization to reproduce protected expression. Public repository excludes restricted third-party materials; do not assume public availability means reusable. No ORC, CC, commercial homebrew license or open-source license selected by this document. Existing rights notices remain intact. Community/commercial terms require explicit owner decision before release.
## Rights register and gate
Fields: asset/source ID, title, creator/rightsholder, origin URL or local reference, version/date, license exact text/reference, permitted uses, attribution, modification/distribution restrictions, evidence, reviewer, status and affected publications. Status unreviewed/pending/cleared/restricted/rejected. Only documented cleared material enters release; original work still records provenance. Unknown license blocks redistribution, not a claim of infringement. Research facts independently expressed, no copying protected text/art. Code dependency and font licenses reviewed separately; preserve notices.
## Clearance procedure
Inventory all manuscript, artwork, code, fonts, maps and research assets; verify primary license terms and scope, record evidence, resolve incompatibility or replace, generate attribution list, review title/trademark in intended markets, obtain legal review where appropriate. No claim of actual trademark clearance or verified external license. CindrixOps fictional appearance optional, lore pending. Release gate requires rights register complete and owner approval of distribution terms. Homebrew import does not confer rights; export checks permitted assets. F37 tracks decisions and changes.
## Deliverables
Rights register template, attribution template, release checklist and unresolved decisions: project license, community/commercial policy, naming clearance, asset-by-asset review. This is governance, not legal advice or a license grant.

## Owner-directed licensing policy — 2026-11-24
The owner directs a D&D-inspired split between designated reusable rules and protected official material, permitting homebrew while retaining copyright and control over redistribution of proprietary expression. This supersedes the earlier unresolved policy direction, not the pending selection of exact legal terms. Establish a separately published Blackbox System Reference Document (SRD), with an explicit license covering only designated content; separate community content and compatibility policy. Official manuscripts outside the licensed scope, setting expression, adventures, artwork, branding and software remain reserved except where separately licensed or legally permitted. Creators retain rights in their original contributions. Proposed commercial compatibility permission limited to licensed material; exact terms and branding permissions require owner approval. No implication of endorsement. Do not adopt D&D text or license by copying; evaluate suitable standard license and legal review. Copyright does not protect abstract game mechanics, ideas or facts as such; license cannot override statutory exceptions or third-party rights. Public repository visibility and platform permissions are not equivalent to an open content license. No actual license grant is made by this policy; prepare SRD scope, notices, community policy and final legal text before release. Existing third-party notices remain controlling.
