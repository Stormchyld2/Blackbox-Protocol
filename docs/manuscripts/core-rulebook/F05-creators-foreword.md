# A Word from the Creator

I have always found the most interesting stories to be those in which ordinary people are confronted with extraordinary circumstances. Not because they were destined for greatness, possessed some remarkable power, or were chosen to save the world, but because something happened that demanded a decision, and they were the ones who had to make it.

Blackbox Protocol was created around that idea.

I wanted to build a roleplaying game in which a person's knowledge, experience, and judgment mattered as much as their ability to face danger. A physician should be as valuable as a soldier. A mechanic, journalist, farmer, or schoolteacher should have opportunities to make a meaningful difference. What a character did before the story began should influence who they are, but it should never determine who they are allowed to become.

That philosophy extends to the world itself. The institutions, corporations, governments, and communities found within Blackbox Protocol are shaped by people with competing interests, responsibilities, and beliefs. Some act with good intentions and make terrible mistakes. Others accomplish remarkable things for reasons that may be less admirable. Understanding those distinctions is part of the experience.

In developing the rules, I wanted realism to give weight to decisions without becoming an obstacle to storytelling. Detail has value when it creates meaningful choices, but it should never come at the expense of the enjoyment of sitting around a table with friends and discovering where a story leads.

Although Blackbox Protocol explores uncertainty, conflict, and the fragility of the systems upon which modern civilization depends, it is not a game about the inevitability of failure. It is equally a game about ingenuity, cooperation, perseverance, and the things people are willing to protect when circumstances become difficult.

The world presented in these pages is a starting point. What happens within it belongs to you and the people with whom you share your table. You may uncover something that was never meant to be found, prevent a catastrophe, build something worth preserving, or simply tell the story of a person trying to make sense of a changing world.

Whatever direction you choose, I hope Blackbox Protocol gives you the freedom to tell stories that remain with you long after the dice have stopped rolling.

Welcome to Blackbox Protocol.
