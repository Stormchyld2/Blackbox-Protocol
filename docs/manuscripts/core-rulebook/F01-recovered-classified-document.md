**SUBJECT:** BLACKBOX PROTOCOL — IMPLEMENTATION AND CONTINUITY

---

Following the conclusion of recent interagency consultations, the Institute has received notification that implementation of the procedures collectively designated **BLACKBOX PROTOCOL** may proceed within the limits established by the participating authorities.

The Institute's responsibilities remain consistent with its original research mandate: the preservation of essential infrastructure, the continuity of lawful decision-making, and the protection of civilian populations during circumstances in which conventional systems of coordination are no longer reliable.

The provisions contained within Annex ███ represent a substantial departure from the recommendations originally submitted by the Institute. Although the revised framework addresses several longstanding operational limitations, concerns regarding accountability, jurisdiction and the termination of exceptional authorizations remain unresolved.

The Institute has formally requested clarification concerning ████████████████████████████████████. At the time of this memorandum, no satisfactory response has been received.

Personnel assigned to the implementation review are reminded that technical capability does not, in itself, constitute lawful authority. Participation in the Protocol does not relieve any individual or institution of responsibility for decisions made under its provisions.

The preservation of an institution must never be confused with the preservation of the people it was established to serve.

Effective immediately, all related research records, correspondence and authorization documents are to be retained under restricted archival custody. Access requests originating outside established channels must be referred to ████████████████.

Further distribution of this memorandum is prohibited without written authorization.
