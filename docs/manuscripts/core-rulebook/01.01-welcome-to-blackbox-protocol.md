# 1.1 — Welcome to Blackbox Protocol

The world is a familiar place. People wake each morning, prepare for work, take their children to school, meet with friends, and concern themselves with the responsibilities of everyday life. Electricity flows through power lines, goods move across continents, information travels almost instantaneously, and institutions provide the services upon which millions of people depend. Most of these systems operate so reliably that few stop to consider the extraordinary degree of cooperation required to keep them functioning.

For most people, tomorrow is expected to resemble today.

But beneath the apparent stability of modern civilization lies a complicated network of competing interests, institutional relationships, technological dependencies, and decisions whose consequences are not always immediately understood. Governments negotiate agreements that may influence generations. Corporations develop technologies that transform entire industries. Researchers pursue discoveries without knowing precisely how their work will eventually be used. Individuals make choices that seem insignificant at the time, only to discover years later that those choices contributed to something much larger.

This is the world of Blackbox Protocol.

## A Familiar World with an Unfamiliar History

Blackbox Protocol is an original tabletop roleplaying game set within an alternate history of our own world. Its geography, cultures, technologies, and many of its historical events will be recognizable. Its cities are populated by people whose lives are shaped by the same ambitions, difficulties, responsibilities, and uncertainties that exist in the world we know.

Yet its history is not entirely our own.

Throughout the latter half of the twentieth century, developments in science, communications, infrastructure, intelligence, and emergency preparedness influenced the relationships between governments, private institutions, and the organizations entrusted with maintaining essential services. Some of these developments became matters of public record. Others remained confined to restricted archives, institutional records, and the memories of individuals whose involvement was never widely understood.

By the beginning of 2026, the consequences of those developments have become woven into the fabric of everyday life. The world continues to function, and most people have little reason to question the history behind the systems they depend upon.

There is no universal conspiracy directing every event. Not every powerful institution conceals wrongdoing, and not every unanswered question has a sinister explanation. The world contains honest officials, responsible corporations, dedicated researchers, opportunists, criminals, and ordinary individuals attempting to fulfill their responsibilities under circumstances they cannot entirely control.

What is happening within that world, what it means, and whether it becomes relevant to your character are matters to be discovered through play.

## Ordinary People in Extraordinary Circumstances

At the center of Blackbox Protocol are people.

Your character might be a detective investigating a disappearance, a physician working within an overwhelmed hospital, an engineer responsible for critical infrastructure, or a journalist pursuing a story that others would prefer remain unpublished. You might portray a business owner, a military veteran, a university researcher, a farmer, a mechanic, or someone whose previous experience offers little preparation for the circumstances they are about to encounter.

Your character does not need to be a member of a secret organization, possess exceptional abilities, or occupy a position of authority to have a meaningful role in the story. Their importance emerges from the decisions they make, the relationships they establish, the knowledge they possess, and their willingness to act when circumstances demand it.

Blackbox Protocol does not assign characters to permanent classes that determine the limits of their development. Instead, a character begins with a background shaped by education, occupation, personal experience, and individual interests. Those experiences establish a foundation upon which the character can continue to develop throughout the campaign.

A paramedic may eventually become an investigator. An electrician may acquire the knowledge necessary to maintain a community's infrastructure. A former police officer may abandon their previous profession and establish a business. A researcher may discover that their most valuable contribution to an unfolding situation has little to do with their original field of study.

These possibilities are not exceptions to the game. They are part of its fundamental design.

Your occupation describes part of your character's history. It does not determine their future.

## More Than a Story of Survival

Although survival is an important element of Blackbox Protocol, the game is not exclusively concerned with catastrophe or societal collapse.

A campaign might follow a group of investigators attempting to establish the truth behind a criminal enterprise. It might concern the personal and professional lives of employees within a major corporation, the development of an independent business, a humanitarian operation, a political controversy, or the consequences of a disaster affecting a single community.

Other campaigns may explore widespread infrastructure failure, economic disruption, displacement, conflict, and the difficulties of maintaining essential services under increasingly challenging circumstances. Some may continue into periods of sustained instability, where characters must adapt to unfamiliar conditions and participate in the reconstruction of communities.

None of these outcomes is inevitable.

A campaign beginning in the contemporary world may remain there indefinitely. A crisis might be contained through competent preparation, effective cooperation, or the intervention of the characters. Institutions may recover from significant failures. Communities may endure circumstances that initially appear beyond their ability to manage.

Even in campaigns where civilization has suffered substantial collapse, the story need not be defined entirely by violence, scarcity, or despair. People continue to establish relationships, raise families, share knowledge, organize communities, and create institutions. The ability to build something lasting can be as significant an achievement as the ability to survive.

Blackbox Protocol provides the framework for these stories without prescribing which of them must occur.

## Knowledge, Judgment, and Consequences

The challenges encountered during play are not always resolved through physical strength or confrontation. Information, experience, preparation, communication, and sound judgment can be equally important.

A character who understands how an institution operates may recognize an inconsistency that others overlook. A person with relevant medical training may identify a problem before it becomes a serious emergency. An experienced negotiator may prevent a confrontation that would otherwise place several people in danger.

The rules provide a consistent means of determining what characters can accomplish when the outcome of an action is uncertain. They also recognize that possessing knowledge does not guarantee success, that favorable circumstances can change, and that decisions may produce consequences extending well beyond their immediate results.

Failure is not necessarily the end of an opportunity. An unsuccessful investigation may reveal that an initial assumption was incorrect. A failed negotiation may change the relationship between two organizations. An unexpected complication may force characters to reconsider their priorities or pursue a different course of action.

Likewise, success does not always mean that a situation has been resolved completely. Accomplishing an immediate objective may introduce new responsibilities, expose previously unknown problems, or influence events elsewhere in the world.

What matters is that the actions of the characters have meaning within the circumstances of the campaign.

## A World That Continues Beyond Your Character

The world of Blackbox Protocol does not exist solely to provide challenges for the player characters.

Individuals pursue personal ambitions. Businesses compete for customers and resources. Organizations respond to changing circumstances. Governments establish policies, communities develop relationships, and institutions sometimes struggle to reconcile their responsibilities with their own interests.

Many of these developments will occur independently of the characters. Some will affect them directly, while others may remain distant events whose significance becomes apparent only later.

Characters may choose to intervene in these developments, benefit from them, oppose them, or remain uninvolved. Their influence will depend upon their circumstances, resources, knowledge, relationships, and the opportunities available to them.

A character investigating misconduct within a local institution may never encounter the larger historical questions surrounding Blackbox Protocol. Another may spend an entire campaign exploring those questions without discovering every answer. Both experiences are equally valid.

The setting provides a foundation for play, not a predetermined sequence of events that every group must experience.

## Your Story Begins Here

The pages that follow introduce the principles, rules, and possibilities of Blackbox Protocol. You will learn how to create a character, determine the outcome of uncertain actions, develop new abilities, interact with the world, and respond to the opportunities and difficulties encountered throughout a campaign.

You do not need specialized knowledge of medicine, law enforcement, engineering, military operations, or any other profession to portray a character with experience in those fields. The rules provide a framework through which your character's knowledge and abilities can become part of the story.

Nor do you need to understand every detail of the setting before beginning. Much of what makes a roleplaying campaign memorable comes from discovering things that neither the players nor their characters initially understood.

Your character may begin with a demanding career, a modest home, a family, a collection of unfinished ambitions, or a determination to leave their previous life behind. Their circumstances may change gradually or without warning. The direction they take is shaped by the choices made throughout play.

There is no predetermined destination.

There is only a world, the people who inhabit it, and the consequences of what they choose to do.
