# What You Need to Play

Getting started with Blackbox Protocol requires very little beyond a group of people, a few basic materials, and a willingness to explore the world through the eyes of your character. Although additional accessories and digital tools can enhance the experience, none are necessary to play.

## The Essentials

**Players and a Game Master**

One participant assumes the role of the Game Master (GM), while the remaining participants create and portray their characters. A group of three to five players and a Game Master is recommended, although the game can accommodate smaller or larger groups.

**Dice**

Blackbox Protocol uses two types of dice:

- **Percentile Dice (D100):** Typically represented by two ten-sided dice, percentile dice are used to resolve skill checks, opposed actions, and other situations in which the outcome is uncertain.
- **Twenty-Sided Die (D20):** Used to resolve damage and certain secondary circumstances as specified by the rules.

Physical dice and digital dice rollers are equally suitable for play.

**Character Sheets**

Each player requires a character sheet to record their character's attributes, skills, competencies, equipment, conditions, and personal development. Character sheets may be printed or maintained digitally.

**Writing Materials**

A pencil, eraser, and notebook are useful for recording information discovered during play, tracking resources, documenting relationships, and keeping a record of important events. Digital notes are equally suitable.

**The Player's Handbook**

This handbook contains the fundamental rules necessary to create characters and participate in a Blackbox Protocol campaign. It also serves as a reference when questions arise during play.

## Optional Play Materials

Depending on the nature of your campaign, the following materials may be useful:

- Maps, miniatures, and character tokens.
- Equipment and inventory cards.
- Evidence cards, photographs, and case files.
- Character journals and campaign records.
- Resource, condition, and environmental trackers.
- A Game Master screen and reference tables.

An investigative campaign may benefit from a case board and organized evidence records, while a survival campaign may make greater use of maps, inventory sheets, and resource trackers. Use the materials that support your campaign without introducing unnecessary bookkeeping.

## Digital Play

Blackbox Protocol is designed to support traditional tabletop play, fully digital sessions, and combinations of the two. Digital dice rollers, character sheets, maps, and campaign management tools may be used to simplify recordkeeping and improve accessibility.

No internet connection, subscription, or dedicated software is required to play the tabletop game.

The most important materials at any table are the imagination, judgment, and participation of the people gathered around it. Everything else exists to support the experience.
