# Layout Suggestions

Internal production reference for the Blackbox Protocol Player's Handbook. This folder is not reader-facing manuscript content. Maintain one document for front matter, each of the seven parts, and back matter. Add section-specific layout and artwork notes here as manuscript development proceeds. Distinguish creator-approved specifications from provisional recommendations; do not treat suggestions as commissioned or completed artwork.

## Established visual direction
- White reading surfaces, black body text, Protocol Red accents.
- Use creator-supplied mirrored left/right page frames; top bar holds the chapter or section title and footer holds the page number. Preserve existing frame, emblem, watermark and red column divider.
- Icons are red or black on transparent backgrounds. Reuse approved dice and icon assets.
- Chapter openings follow the supplied cinematic chapter-page concept: chapter number/title, thematic artwork, introductory passage and numbered section contents with icons where appropriate.
- Reserve heavy distressed effects for covers, chapter openings, frames and fictional artifacts; keep ordinary reading areas clear.
- Existing front/back cover art and proposed chapter-opening art are visual references. Earlier approximate conceptual palette: carbon black #101316, red #D50910, steel gray #B8B7B2, dossier paper #E1D1B3, off-white #F2F0EA. Ordinary reading surfaces are now white, overriding earlier cream proposals. Exact print palette, typefaces, margins and proofs are not finalized.
- Approximately 350 interior pages, excluding cover; front matter target 10 pages. Page allocations are working estimates, not fixed pagination.
- D&D 2024 PHB, Pathfinder 2e Remastered Player Core, Call of Cthulhu 7e Keeper Rulebook and Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay 4e Core Rulebook are secondary presentation benchmarks only. Do not copy protected text or assets.
- Proposed typography: heavy distressed uppercase chapter titles, white bold uppercase bar labels, black primary headings, red secondary headings, readable black body type, high-contrast tables and smaller captions. Exact fonts remain open.
- Proposed production format: 8.5 x 11 inches, primarily two columns, full color, 10.5–11 pt body text, 20–25% artwork coverage, 100,000–125,000 words. These are recommendations, not creator-locked values.
- Page numbers at outer footer ends are a suggestion, not a confirmed change to supplied templates.
- Reuse scalable red/black frames, dice illustrations, icons and corporate logos where relevant; ensure contrast, grayscale legibility, labels and accessible digital equivalents. Avoid placing decorative elements behind dense text.
