# How to Use This Book

The Blackbox Protocol Player's Handbook is both an introduction to the game and a reference for use throughout your campaigns. It is not necessary to read every chapter before your first session. Begin with the fundamentals, learn how to create your character, and refer to the remaining chapters as situations arise during play.

## The Organization of This Book

**Part I — How to Play**  
Introduces the game, the responsibilities of players and the Game Master, and the fundamental rules used to resolve actions and determine outcomes.

**Part II — Characters**  
Guides you through character creation, explaining attributes, skills, occupations, competencies, personal history and relationships.

**Part III — Playing the Game**  
Explains how characters interact with the world, conduct investigations, engage in conflict, manage injuries and psychological pressure, and respond to environmental hazards.

**Part IV — Gear, Money and Travel**  
Covers equipment, inventory, finances, transportation, crafting, repairs and the practical resources characters depend upon.

**Part V — Development**  
Explains how characters acquire knowledge, improve their abilities, undertake training and pursue new occupations throughout their lives.

**Part VI — Running the Game**  
Introduces the principles of adjudication, campaign configuration and optional rules that allow groups to adapt the game to their preferred style of play.

**Part VII — The Blackbox Protocol Era**  
Introduces the contemporary setting, its history, institutions, organizations and opportunities for adventure.

The appendices contain reference charts, character resources, a glossary and an index for locating information during play.

## Understanding the Rules

Blackbox Protocol uses a shared mechanical foundation throughout its three eras. Standard rules provide the complete core experience, while Advanced and Simulation options introduce additional detail where appropriate. Quick Start and Casual configurations simplify the experience, and Custom configurations allow groups to select their preferred level of detail across individual systems.

Optional rules are identified separately from standard procedures. Your Game Master will establish which options are applicable to your campaign.

Throughout this book, examples demonstrate how rules are applied during play. Cross-references identify related sections and, where applicable, the corresponding rule and edition.

## Beyond This Handbook

The Player's Handbook establishes the fundamental rules of Blackbox Protocol. Companion publications expand upon investigation, equipment, survival, campaign management and other specialized subjects.

Signal Lost and Dead Zone explore additional eras within the same world, while the Transition Handbook addresses the continuity of characters and campaigns between them.

These publications build upon the same rules rather than replacing them. You do not need to collect every supplementary publication before beginning your first campaign.

Whether you are learning the game for the first time or returning to resolve a particular situation, this handbook is intended to remain a useful companion throughout your character's development.
