# F18 Economics and Trade

Canonical DESIGN-NOTES F18; existing F04–F14 mechanics remain authoritative. Delegated working foundation, not final publication or human-tested rules.

## Markets and value
Market ID, location, date, accepted currencies/units, stock, demand, price quote, validity, access and counterparties. Blackbox Protocol era uses actual local currency (2026 USD for the US baseline); Signal Lost permits mixed currency and exchange where accepted; Dead Zone defaults to local accounting/barter. There is no universal exchange rate. Prices are local explicit quotes, not inferred from era. Standard GM affordability and agreed exchange; Advanced quantities and regional quotes; Simulation production/labor/transport and trends. Currency ownership persists when acceptance disappears.
## Transactions
Quote quantity×unit price in integer smallest currency units; explicit fees and rounding at quote stage. Validate funds, stock, acceptance, access and consent before atomic exchange; reject insufficient balances without mutation. Barter records both item legs and agreed quantities, no forced equal nominal value. Credit records principal, currency/unit, parties, due date, terms and repayment; no automatic interest absent agreement. Employment records compensation, work interval and obligations; F11 time, F17 stock. No duplicated income or sale of reserved stock.
## Starting allowance and calibration
Contemporary US starting cash uses a **flat $2,500 baseline per character**, with a GM-approved, history-justified variation limited to **$2,000–$3,000**. Occupation determines recurring monthly income based on actual employment and setting-appropriate pay; monthly living costs, debt payments and obligations are tracked separately. Significant assets and occupational equipment are recorded separately and are not free cash. No salary table is implied by the cash baseline; a monthly pay reference remains to be authored or GM-set. This owner-confirmed 2026-11-26 decision supersedes the older history-only starting-cash wording in DESIGN-NOTES §7.2. Signal Lost and Dead Zone starting resources still depend on actual surviving assets, access and local economy, not an automatic $2,500 grant. Optional Means is a 0–6 alternative to tracking currency for a character, never combined with actual currency for that character. Delegated arithmetic fixture only: 3 units at 400 cents each cost 1,200 cents; a balance of 2,000 cents becomes 800 cents. This fixture is not a price-list entry. The existing catalogue contains estimated fictional-product reference prices in integer cents, and its price_tier values follow the Means 0–6 brackets in DESIGN-NOTES 7.2. These are not researched real-product prices. Signal Lost scarcity guidance is plentiful ×1, short ×2–5, scarce ×5–20, unavailable = no sale, subject to GM-established local markets. F36 calibration remains pending. GM may set local prices before play; no fabricated universal economics. Market/trade/debt/employment sheets, transaction IDs and audit trail.
