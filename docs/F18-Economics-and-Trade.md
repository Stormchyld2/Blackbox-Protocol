# F18 Economics and Trade

Canonical DESIGN-NOTES F18; existing F04–F14 mechanics remain authoritative. Delegated working foundation, not final publication or human-tested rules.

## Markets and value
Market ID, location, date, accepted currencies/units, stock, demand, price quote, validity, access and counterparties. BP conventional currency, SL mixed exchange, DZ local accounting/barter; no universal exchange rate. Prices are local explicit quotes, not inferred from era. Standard GM affordability and agreed exchange; Advanced quantities and regional quotes; Simulation production/labor/transport and trends. Currency ownership persists when acceptance disappears.
## Transactions
Quote quantity×unit price in integer smallest currency units; explicit fees and rounding at quote stage. Validate funds, stock, acceptance, access and consent before atomic exchange; reject insufficient balances without mutation. Barter records both item legs and agreed quantities, no forced equal nominal value. Credit records principal, currency/unit, parties, due date, terms and repayment; no automatic interest absent agreement. Employment records compensation, work interval and obligations; F11 time, F17 stock. No duplicated income or sale of reserved stock.
## Starting allowance and calibration
Existing equal campaign purchasing allowance in local terms; occupational equipment separate, no fixed universal starting cash. Delegated sample market fixture only: 3 units at 4 fictional credits costs12; balance20 becomes8. Price tables, scarcity curves and actual equipment costs require researched catalogue and F36 calibration. GM may set local prices before play; no fabricated universal economics. Market/trade/debt/employment sheets, transaction IDs and audit trail.
