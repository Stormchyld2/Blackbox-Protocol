Round procedure

Each participant: one main action and one movement allowance in either order, plus one reaction. Main action may become a second move, never a second reaction. Unused actions expire. Ties: higher effective PER, then simultaneous. Recalculate initiative next round after relevant changes.

Defense: an aware defender may spend the reaction on an opposed DEX (evasion) or Close Combat check, chosen before dice. Attacker wins only if defense does not win or tie. Range bands: engaged / near / far / out of reach. Cover −10 partial, −20 substantial.

At 0 HP after severe or critical harm: unstable. Mark one deterioration box at the end of each later round; death at three. Stabilize: helper's main action, Medicine check, one medical supply use.

Demand: 1 food and 1 water ration per character per day. Each unmet category costs 5 stamina (half ration 2). Counter +1 for each unmet day, −1 for each fully met day, minimum 0.

Deprived (water 2+, food 4+): −10 physical, no HP recovery. Severely deprived (water 3+, food 7+): −20 physical and sustained concentration, no HP recovery, sleep restores no stamina. Critical (water 5+, food 21+): −30 or infeasible; GM may declare a life-threatening condition.

Ammunition: one use per encounter with ranged fire (Standard). Medical: one use per stabilization or daily care assessment. Repair: one use per repair project.
