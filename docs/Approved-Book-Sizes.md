# Blackbox Protocol — Publication & Artwork Standards

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved
**Scope:** All Blackbox Protocol rulebooks, expansions, and sourcebooks.

## 1. Publication Specifications

| Specification | Standard |
|---|---|
| Finished page size | 8.5 × 11 inches (US Letter) |
| Orientation | Portrait |
| Full-page resolution | 2550 × 3300 pixels |
| Image resolution | 300 PPI |
| Page background | Pure White (#FFFFFF) |
| Primary colors | Red, Black, White |
| Standard layout | Two columns |
| Artwork format | PNG |
| Final publication format | Print-ready PDF |

## 2. Artwork Dimensions

| Artwork Type | Target Dimensions |
|---|---|
| Full-page | 2550 × 3300 px |
| Half-page horizontal | 2550 × 1650 px |
| Half-page vertical | 1275 × 3300 px |
| Quarter-page | 1275 × 1650 px |
| Character portraits | Sized according to placement |
| Equipment illustrations | Sized according to placement |
| Icons and emblems | Transparent background |

All raster artwork must maintain an effective resolution of
300 PPI at its final printed size.

These dimensions are nominal targets before margins, gutters,
and bleed are applied.

## 3. Standard Interior Pages

- Pure white background.
- Mirrored left-hand and right-hand master pages.
- Black-and-red header and footer bars.
- Original Blackbox Protocol logo in the header.
- Original BP emblem centered in the footer.
- Thin red vertical divider between text columns.
- Subtle aperture watermark at approximately 5–7% opacity.
- No weathered page edges.
- No decorative side rails.
- Clean, sharp geometric accents.
- Consistent header and footer positioning.

### Left-Hand Page

- Blackbox Protocol logo positioned at the upper left.
- Header accent positioned at the upper right.
- Aperture watermark positioned at the lower right.

### Right-Hand Page

- Blackbox Protocol logo positioned at the upper right.
- Header accent positioned at the upper left.
- Aperture watermark positioned at the lower left.

Both templates must be exact geometric counterparts wherever
possible. Logos must retain their original reading orientation.

## 4. Title Pages

- Pure white background.
- Original Blackbox Protocol logo.
- Prominent publication title.
- Cinematic artwork representing the publication's subject.
- Artwork edges transitioning naturally into white.
- Black-and-red header and footer accents.
- Centered BP footer emblem.
- Subtle aperture watermark.
- No weathered outer edges or decorative side rails.

## 5. Chapter Opening Pages

- Pure white background.
- Original Blackbox Protocol logo centered in the header.
- Prominent chapter number and title.
- Chapter-specific cinematic illustration.
- Artwork transitioning naturally into white.
- Introductory chapter text.
- Chapter contents or section overview.
- Consistent section icons.
- Subtle aperture watermark.
- Chapter title and page number in the footer.
- Original BP emblem centered in the footer.

Chapter opening mockups establish visual direction only.
Final text and section titles must match the approved manuscript.

## 6. Branding and Logo Standards

- Always use the original approved logo assets.
- Place logos directly as separate image layers.
- Never regenerate or redraw official logos using AI.
- Never stretch, distort, or reverse logo lettering.
- Maintain original logo proportions and colors.
- Use transparent PNG assets where appropriate.
- Preserve continuous header and footer bars behind logos.
- Remove unintended gaps, artifacts, and irregular edges.
- Maintain consistent logo sizing across equivalent templates.

## 7. Color Standards

| Color | Usage |
|---|---|
| White (#FFFFFF) | Page backgrounds |
| Black | Body text, bars, headings |
| Red | Branding, accents, dividers, highlights |
| Light Gray | Watermarks and subtle backgrounds |

Exact red and secondary color values remain pending approval.

Warm cream and weathered parchment are not approved as
standard interior page backgrounds.

## 8. Artwork Direction

- Cinematic realism.
- Modern, grounded visual presentation.
- Detailed and believable environments.
- Realistic character proportions.
- Consistent lighting and rendering quality.
- Clean edges and transitions.
- Appropriate imagery for the applicable game era.
- Consistent representation of fictional organizations,
  equipment, technology, and branding.

Artwork must reflect the applicable setting:

- Blackbox Protocol — Contemporary world.
- Signal Lost — Emerging crisis and societal disruption.
- Dead Zone — Post-collapse survival.

## 9. Typography and Text Layout

- Standard interior pages use a two-column layout.
- Chapter titles use bold industrial typography.
- Body text prioritizes readability.
- Heading hierarchies must remain consistent.
- Tables and reference charts must remain legible.
- Manuscript text must remain editable.
- Do not embed final manuscript text into generated artwork.
- Fonts must be licensed for commercial publication.
- Fonts must be embedded in final publication PDFs.

Exact font families, sizes, and spacing remain pending approval.

## 10. Production Quality Control

Before approving any final page or artwork:

- [ ] Correct page dimensions.
- [ ] Effective resolution of at least 300 PPI.
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio.
- [ ] Original approved logos used.
- [ ] Correct logo placement and orientation.
- [ ] Consistent branding and colors.
- [ ] Clean edges without unintended artifacts.
- [ ] No distorted typography.
- [ ] No unintended white gaps in graphic elements.
- [ ] Correct header and footer alignment.
- [ ] Consistent watermark opacity.
- [ ] Correct chapter and page numbering.
- [ ] Text checked against the approved manuscript.
- [ ] Adequate printing and binding margins.
- [ ] Commercial usage rights verified.
- [ ] Final PDF visually inspected.

## 12. Design Governance

These standards apply to all Blackbox Protocol publications.

Approved master page templates must not be independently
regenerated or redesigned without explicit approval.

All new page designs, illustrations, tables, icons, and
publication assets must conform to this document.

Any deviation from the approved visual standards requires
approval from the project owner.
