# F14 — Psychological Mechanics

Fictional game mechanics, not clinical assessment or advice. Canonical DESIGN-NOTES Section4.4, F03 player agency, F06 derived attributes, F11 clock, F13 physical recovery. No new numerical defaults.

## Pressure and resilience
Stress is integer0–100, initially0 unless agreed history. P=floor((effective WIL+effective CON)/2) is resilience reference, not a spendable pool. HP and stamina remain separate. GM establishes meaningful distinct pressure event, intensity low5/moderate10/high20 and stakes before one WIL check using R01–R03. Routine or unchanged repeated stimulus does not trigger repeated checks. Possible uncertain checks clamp1–99; natural01 critical success,100 critical failure. Ordinary success adds floor(intensity/2); strong, exceptional or critical success adds0; failure adds intensity; critical failure adds intensity+5. Clamp stress0–100. No additional pressure die.

## Bands and functional effects
For living characters use thresholds at least1: strained=max(1,ceil(P/2)), pressured=max(1,P), overwhelmed=max(1,min(100,P+25)). Highest applicable band wins, including coincident thresholds. Below strained composed, strained -5, pressured -10, overwhelmed -20. Apply only to materially affected tasks; no duplicate WIL impairment. Reevaluate bands after resilience changes without automatically changing stress. Player chooses plausible response: pause, seek support, withdraw or continue with applicable penalty. No compulsory violence, arbitrary loss of agency or diagnosis.

## Recovery and support
Safe eligible quiet hour reduces5; adequate eight-hour sleep reduces20 instead of hourly stress recovery for same interval. Clamp at0. Consensual supportive interaction with appropriate skill may reduce additional5 once per day on success. No repeated farming or recovery during ongoing acute threat. F11 records elapsed intervals, evidence and unique outcome IDs. F13 stamina/HP recovery remains separate; eligible rest can update independent pools but not duplicate the same stress award. Failed support does not automatically increase stress. Resource and support availability depend on fiction, not era label.

## Persistent conditions and safety
Advanced persistent condition requires player and GM agreement following major event or sustained overwhelmed state; record specific fictional triggers, functional effects, review date and agreed support. Qualified support and time can modify or resolve through agreed reassessment; no fixed clinical timetable or automatic mental illness. Simulation adds longitudinal tracking and support availability, not compulsory extra rolls. Respect table safety boundaries, allow omission or abstraction of distressing content, and preserve player control of character personality. Anomalies in F25 do not automatically impose sanity loss. These procedural safeguards elaborate existing agency principles under delegated authority.

## Modes and records
Standard stress and current band; Advanced distinct events and agreed persistent conditions; Simulation longitudinal record and resource access. Same numeric rules. Record character, event ID, intensity, declared stakes, WIL target, roll/category, prior/result stress, resilience, applicable band, affected tasks, recovery interval ID, support date and consent, condition review and GM adjudication. Keep sensitive fictional notes access-controlled by table agreement; F31–35 own implementation. No automatic reset on era or mode transition.

## Analytical balance
