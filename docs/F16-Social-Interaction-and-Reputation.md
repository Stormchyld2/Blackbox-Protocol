# F16 Social Interaction and Reputation

Canonical DESIGN-NOTES F16; existing F04–F14 mechanics remain authoritative. Delegated working foundation, not final publication or human-tested rules.

## Social resolution
Record NPC goals, boundaries, relationship, trust, obligations, affiliation, contextual reputation and actual authority separately. Declare intent, leverage and plausible outcome; routine agreement no roll, impossible concession no roll, uncertain interaction uses existing Persuasion/Intimidation/Deception/Leadership or justified attribute. Opposed checks use R01–R03. Success achieves feasible declared outcome, not mind control or hidden truth. Failure changes only justified consequences; no repeated unchanged requests for rerolls. Players control their own beliefs and choices.
## State and authority
Delegated qualitative attitude scale hostile/wary/neutral/receptive/supportive, not numeric modifier ladder. Change only with evidenced events and GM adjudication; no automatic shift per successful roll. Reputation scoped to audience, location and topic, with source and date; rumors distinguished from verified deeds. Authority records issuer, scope, validity, restrictions; expertise survives loss of office. Obligations record parties, terms, due date, fulfillment/dispute. No universal morality score or automatic career grant.
## Modes and records
Standard qualitative NPC cards; Advanced relationship and faction records; Simulation evolving networks and obligations. Example former official retains expertise but lacks authority in a new community. Record event ID and before/after state, visibility, rationale; F23 owns faction mechanics, F18 debts, F24 propagation. F36 tests agency and social pacing.
