# INTRODUCING BLACKBOX PROTOCOL

**The world changes. The rules of survival don't.**

Blackbox Protocol is an upcoming tabletop roleplaying game built around human capability, uncertainty, and consequence.

There are no character classes dictating who you must become. Your occupation, knowledge, skills, training, and decisions define your character.

From the complexities of the contemporary world to the uncertainty of societal collapse and what follows, Blackbox Protocol offers three distinct eras connected by a single rules system.

- Classless character creation
- Skill-based progression
- Grounded, consequential combat
- Psychological and survival mechanics
- Open-world campaigns
- Three playable eras

You don't have to be a hero. You don't have to save the world.

You have to decide what matters when everything is on the line.
