# Anomaly catalogue — twelve original anomalies (approved direction, 2026-11-25)

Status: **Approved by Jason as the working catalogue.** Original concepts; descriptions may be refined in the Anomaly Compendium. Owning book: Anomaly Compendium. Optional module (F25): the core game is complete without it, and every anomaly has a grounded reading.

## Template (F25 dossier, existing rules only)
Each entry has: category, observable signs, triggers, effects, limits, discovery routes (at least two independent, F15), counterplay, and **three readings**: *Grounded* (a mundane explanation the GM can choose), *Ambiguous* (evidence supports more than one view; the GM may leave it open), *Optional supernatural* (only if the group opts in). Effects use only Section 4.3 condition penalties (−10/−20/−30), Section 4.4 pressure events (low 5, moderate 10, high 20; one check per distinct event, no repeated farming) and Section 4.2 harm profiles. No new dice, no sanity track, no automatic condition (F25). GM truth stays out of handouts.

## Psychological
**A-01 The Insistent Memory.** *Signs:* several unconnected people recall the same detailed event that no record supports. *Trigger:* exposure to a specific location or object. *Effect:* moderate pressure event on discovering the contradiction; −10 to tasks needing confident recall until resolved. *Limits:* only memory, never behaviour control; affects people who touch the object. *Discovery:* conflicting witness statements; the object traced to a common source. *Counterplay:* remove the object; document and cross-check records. *Grounded:* suggestion and a viral false story. *Ambiguous:* a group belief that has taken on a life of its own. *Supernatural:* the place keeps an imprint.

**A-02 Shared Sleep.** *Signs:* a group dreams the same setting for a week. *Trigger:* shared shelter or proximity. *Effect:* low pressure event nightly; the 4.4 sleep stress recovery is halved while it lasts. *Limits:* ends when the group separates for a few days. *Discovery:* dream journals; a shared environmental cause such as noise or a gas leak. *Counterplay:* change shelter, separate, seek medical review. *Grounded:* shared stressor plus conversation. *Ambiguous:* an unknown environmental trigger. *Supernatural:* a place or presence reaching out.

## Technological
**A-03 The Answering Line.** *Signs:* an out-of-service phone or radio line answers with partial voices. *Trigger:* dialing a specific number or tuning a frequency at night. *Effect:* moderate pressure event the first time; no harm. *Limits:* audio only, certain devices only. *Discovery:* call logs, device teardown, spectrum scan. *Counterplay:* disconnect, trace, shield. *Grounded:* crosstalk and a buffer replaying old calls. *Ambiguous:* an unexplained signal source. *Supernatural:* the dead speaking.

**A-04 Ghost Schedule.** *Signs:* automated systems run tasks nobody scheduled, at the same hour. *Trigger:* one building's control system. *Effect:* resource drain or misdirected machinery; harm only if people stand in the way (4.2 environmental, high). *Limits:* confined to one network. *Discovery:* system logs; maintenance records. *Counterplay:* isolate or reset the system. *Grounded:* an orphan script and a clock fault. *Ambiguous:* tampering by an unknown party. *Supernatural:* a will in the machine.

## Biological
**A-05 The Quiet Bloom.** *Signs:* an unfamiliar fungus-like growth spreads in damp rooms; people nearby feel lethargic. *Trigger:* extended stay in the affected rooms. *Effect:* −10 to physical tasks after repeated days, and recovery blocked while exposed. *Limits:* needs humidity; dies in dry conditions. *Discovery:* sample analysis; local medical clustering. *Counterplay:* dry and ventilate, remove material, seek care. *Grounded:* ordinary mold of an unusual species. *Ambiguous:* a novel organism. *Supernatural:* something feeding on the building.

**A-06 Hollow Hive.** *Signs:* an apiary or colony abandons its home overnight with food stores intact. *Trigger:* a nearby specific plant or chemical. *Effect:* resource loss; stings if disturbed (low harm). *Limits:* affects only insect colonies and a few animals. *Discovery:* the site and field notes; a plant survey. *Counterplay:* remove the source, relocate colonies. *Grounded:* collapse from pests or chemicals. *Ambiguous:* an unidentified toxin. *Supernatural:* the colonies fled something unseen.

## Environmental
**A-07 Whiteout Pocket.** *Signs:* a patch of terrain where fog and sound dampen unpredictably and navigation gear misreads. *Trigger:* entering at certain temperature and wind. *Effect:* getting lost (Navigation at −20), stress if separated. *Limits:* fixed area; ends when conditions change. *Discovery:* local guides; weather and terrain survey. *Counterplay:* markers, rope lines, avoidance. *Grounded:* microclimate and magnetic ore. *Ambiguous:* an unusual atmospheric effect. *Supernatural:* ground that wants visitors.

**A-08 The Long Echo.** *Signs:* a valley repeats sounds seconds late and altered. *Trigger:* a loud sound inside the valley. *Effect:* low pressure event; may mask real danger sounds (Search −10). *Limits:* acoustic only. *Discovery:* sound recording and mapping. *Counterplay:* avoid, or use non-sound signals. *Grounded:* terrain acoustics. *Ambiguous:* an unexplained delay. *Supernatural:* the valley remembering.

## Paranormal
**A-09 Return Visitors.** *Signs:* a closed house is entered at night; items are moved, nothing is taken. *Trigger:* an anniversary or a family member's presence. *Effect:* moderate pressure event; no harm. *Limits:* only moves objects, never attacks. *Discovery:* cameras, logs, neighbour accounts. *Counterplay:* secure the house, investigate, talk to family. *Grounded:* a hidden resident or sleepwalker. *Ambiguous:* unexplained motion. *Supernatural:* a lingering presence.

**A-10 The Counting House.** *Signs:* an archive keeps correct counts of things that should be unknowable. *Trigger:* asking it a count. *Effect:* an information advantage with a declared cost (time, a favor); high pressure event if the count concerns a person. *Limits:* answers numbers only. *Discovery:* records, repeated queries. *Counterplay:* stop using it, destroy records. *Grounded:* an unusually clever filing system. *Ambiguous:* a hidden informant. *Supernatural:* an entity that counts.

## Cosmic
**A-11 Borrowed Hours.** *Signs:* clocks in one building disagree with the outside by exactly one hour, growing daily. *Trigger:* staying inside long enough. *Effect:* confusion and schedule failures; moderate pressure event on realization. *Limits:* returns to normal on leaving. *Discovery:* synchronized clocks and logs. *Counterplay:* leave, use outside timekeeping. *Grounded:* a faulty network time source. *Ambiguous:* unexplained timing drift. *Supernatural:* time bending around the place.

**A-12 The Wrong Sky.** *Signs:* observers see the same unfamiliar star pattern for a few minutes on clear nights. *Trigger:* a specific night and location. *Effect:* high pressure event for those who study it; no physical harm. *Limits:* visual only; does not recur on schedule. *Discovery:* photographs, telescope data, sky charts. *Counterplay:* record it, avoid dwelling on it. *Grounded:* an atmospheric or satellite effect. *Ambiguous:* an unidentified object. *Supernatural:* a sky from somewhere else.
