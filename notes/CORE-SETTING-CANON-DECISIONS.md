# CORE SETTING CANON — APPROVED DECISIONS AND INTEGRATION STATUS
Date: 2026-11-25
Scope: Blackbox Protocol Core, contemporary default year 2026.
Status: The **decisions** below are approved; any newly proposed named incidents/NPCs in other drafts remain provisional until reconciled.

## Identity
Blackbox Protocol Core is a globally playable, classless, skill-based, open-world tabletop RPG with a recognizable contemporary world altered by a plausible alternate history. It is a **story-rich geopolitical, investigative and adventure setting**, not merely a modern-life simulation and not a compulsory conspiracy campaign.

The world is **morally neutral**. Government agencies, military units, corporations, communities and other institutions contain both ethical and corrupt individuals. Institutions are not intrinsically villains. Corruption, legitimate national security, accountability, humanitarian success, and ordinary life coexist. A rumor is not evidence and not every mystery is a conspiracy.

Campaigns may involve civilian exploration, journalism, law, public service, rescue, corporate employment, criminal investigation, diplomacy, private security, intelligence, military operations or command. Players choose their objectives. No single campaign track is mandatory.

## Alternate history and classified legacy
- Project Looking Glass: conceptual origins in 1962, with Cold War continuity planning and a later Blackbox Architecture.
- **1991**: principal historical divergence and formal activation of the **Blackbox Initiative**, initially intended to protect essential continuity during geopolitical emergencies.
- **2001–2015**: gradual emergence of the designation **Blackbox Protocol** for sensitive procedures and a widening institutional legacy. Preserve the name evolution; do not retroactively rename the 1991 initiative.
- **2016–2026**: contested legacy, oversight and reform, corporate and state competition, and fragments of classified history. The program is not omnipotent and does not control all events.
- The GM-only historical foundation includes Continuity Zero, but its present operational status can be reserved for campaign treatment. Reconcile details against the master alternate-history and GM Secret History manuscripts before publication.

## Real places and fictional events
Real countries, cities, geographic features, and military installations may be used in fictional settings, including **Clarksville, Tennessee**, and **Fort Campbell, Kentucky**. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement or real misconduct. Invented officers, missions, corruption incidents and conspiracies should be clearly fictional. Prefer fictional private organizations and personnel for significant wrongdoing. Preserve geographic and institutional accuracy where feasible, and add a standard alternate-history fiction disclaimer to publications.

## West Virginia starting town
**Briar Hollow, West Virginia** is the approved fictional town name, replacing the working title 'The Quiet Town' / 'The Quiet County'. The county is intentionally **unnamed**. Stories may involve countywide government, courts, law enforcement, infrastructure and neighboring areas without establishing a real or fictional county name. The town's detailed history, geography, characters and scenarios require further authoring and review.

## Global reach
The United States receives detailed initial coverage, but the setting must support campaigns worldwide, including the Philippines and other regions with locally grounded perspectives, agency, institutions and histories. Avoid treating foreign countries solely as adversaries or scenery.

## Era dates and editorial firewall
- **Core:** 2026–2030 default setting period.
- **Signal Lost:** starts **2031**.
- **Dead Zone:** starts **2046**, **15 years after Signal Lost begins**.
These dates are approved metadata, **not authorization to write the future eras' lore in Core documents**.
The Core Player's Handbook describes a functioning but unsettled world, without revealing a destined collapse. The Core GM Handbook may conclude with classified, uncertain warning signs late in Core; no fixed cause or inevitability is established. Core campaigns can continue independently.

## Documentation policy — comprehensive, additive, and source-preserving (approved)
**This file is a decision register, not a substitute for any manuscript, guide, or source document.** Blackbox Protocol is to be developed as a comprehensive tabletop role-playing game publishing library. Do not replace substantive history, mechanics, setting lore, or campaign content with short summaries, compressed master documents, outlines, or a single consolidated narrative. Maintain full-length, purpose-specific documents with all established detail intact.

- **Alternate-history manuscript:** Preserve and develop the complete chronology, causes, people, institutions, political developments, regional consequences, conflicting perspectives, and event-level detail.
- **Core World Guide:** Expand each relevant region, real city, fictional settlement, faction, corporation, and institution with geography, history, culture, ordinary life, points of interest, relationships, and player-facing opportunities.
- **Core GM Handbook:** Fully develop classified history, competing interpretations, motivations, hidden relationships, investigative evidence, mysteries, adjudication support, campaign structures, and GM-only late-Core warning signs.
- **Core Player's Handbook:** Provide substantive public history and setting context without exposing classified GM revelations or treating later-era outcomes as inevitable.
- **Campaign/location guides and adventures:** Build Briar Hollow and subsequent locations with detailed establishments, residents, maps or map specifications, timelines, encounters, clues, handouts, branching consequences, and complete playable scenarios—not just three-session outlines.
- **Rules and equipment:** Preserve authoritative source rules, complete records, and cross-references; never silently compress or overwrite them in setting edits.

**Editing procedure:** Before changing a manuscript, inspect the relevant existing files and their relationships. Make additive, narrowly scoped revisions that preserve established material and distinguish approved canon from proposals. When contradictions exist, document and resolve them explicitly rather than deleting either version without review. Maintain separate player-safe and GM-secret treatments where required. A high-level index, summary, or decision log may be useful for navigation but must never replace the underlying comprehensive documents.

**Integration caveat:** The full historical manuscripts discussed in conversation must be obtained and checked before their integration can be called complete. This policy commits the editorial standard; it does not assert that absent manuscripts or full adventure modules have already been authored.
