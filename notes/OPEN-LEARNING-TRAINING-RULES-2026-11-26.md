# Open learning and competency advancement — approved synchronization

**Approved 2026-11-26.** Open learning is an access route, not a separate hours economy or automatic competency grant. The same level-specific hour targets, including compressed complexity 6–7 levels I–IV, apply regardless of occupation, formal school, or independent learning.

## Qualifying modes
- Independent reading, self-study, research and appropriate guided manuals: **1.00×** effective credit per actual qualifying hour.
- Structured supervised practice: **1.25×**.
- Documented relevant hands-on field application, including independently undertaken practice: **1.50×**. Formal employment or an occupational title is not required.
- Qualified supervised fieldwork with actionable feedback: **1.75×**.
- Exceptional approved intensive structured learning: **2.00×**. Requires appropriate resources, qualified supervision, measurable objectives, evidence and advance GM authorization.

The multipliers describe the actual learning activity, not the learner's status. Independent learners can move between modes when the activity qualifies. They cannot claim mentored/intensive credit without the required mentor/program. No stacking with retired prerequisite-surplus discounts.

## Downtime and field ledger
Credit documented relevant hours during safe travel, base operations, field missions, independent experimentation and dedicated downtime. Record actual duration, mode, competency, evidence and approving GM; do not double-count overlapping activities, apply hours to multiple projects, or grant free milestone hours. Inactive or paused projects retain earned credit. Real availability and survival demands still constrain time.

## Advancement
Open learners must satisfy the same sequential level, supporting prerequisites (or approved scoped equivalency), resources, safety, mandatory actual supervised/credential hours where specified, and competency-specific assessments. Accumulated hours establish eligibility to attempt assessment, not automatic proficiency, CDR, professional licensing, or institutional authorization. Neither career label nor formal school is a blanket prerequisite unless a specific competency's access rules genuinely require it. No global CDR level gates.
