# Approved training progression revision — 2026-11-26

**Status: approved design rule; replaces conflicting training-time defaults in CCDD-19/20/22 and MC-03.** Implementations and catalogue hour rows must be migrated and regression-tested before claiming executable parity.

## Base hours
Incremental training target at level t (I=1…V=5): U(c,t)=H(c) × 2^(t−1), absent an explicit catalogue override. New H(c): complexity 1=4, 2=8, 3=16, 4=40, 5=80, 6=100, 7=180 hours. Level I–V cumulative: c1=124, c2=248, c3=496, c4=1240, c5=2480, c6=3100, c7=5580 hours. At complexity 7, level V alone is 2880 raw hours, or 1440 effective real hours with exceptional 200% credit. At 6–8 qualifying hours per active day this is 240–180 active days, not elapsed calendar days.

## Learning-credit modes
Training progress is tracked as **effective hours**, not necessarily elapsed clock hours. Standard structured study: 1.00 effective hour per actual hour; supervised practical training: 1.25; relevant documented field application: 1.50; qualified supervised field application with actionable feedback: 1.75; exceptional structured intensive training: 2.00. Exceptional credit requires advance GM approval, appropriate resources, qualified supervision, measurable objectives and a verified record. Modes are mutually exclusive per actual hour; no stacking, double booking, or applying the same hour to multiple projects. Mode must be justified by the target's competency scope. No credit for trivial repetition or mere presence. These multipliers are **alternatives to**, not cumulative with, the older advanced/simulation surplus discount. The older discount is retired for new projects to prevent double acceleration; existing projects retain their snapshotted target unless voluntarily migrated with no lost earned progress.

## Time ledger and blended downtime
Log actual available time after sleep, work, survival obligations, travel safety and recovery. Dedicated study, safe travel study, base maintenance, operational duties and mission fieldwork may all generate progress only when relevant and feasible. Split an interval among projects, never duplicate actual time; travel or duties cannot grant full training credit without actual qualifying practice. Record source, actual hours, mode, effective hours, competency ID, evidence and approver. Fractional effective hours may accumulate. Interrupted periods retain progress. No fixed uninterrupted block or minimum session duration. Campaign milestones may document qualifying experience but award no free hours without recorded actual qualifying time; assessments can be scheduled at milestones.

## Certification and safeguards
Hours make a character **eligible for assessment**, not automatically proficient. Sequential target levels, supporting prerequisites or approved target-specific equivalency, tools, resources, supervision, demonstrations and assessments still apply. No clinical, engineering, legal or professional authorization is inferred from game training. Mandatory non-discountable supervised or credential durations remain actual elapsed requirements and are not multiplied. Failed assessments retain eligible credited hours and require corrective practice. CDR awards, competency task bonuses, slot thresholds and removal of global CDR eligibility gates are unchanged. Occupational starting grants are unchanged. Project capacity and pause/resume rules remain unchanged.
