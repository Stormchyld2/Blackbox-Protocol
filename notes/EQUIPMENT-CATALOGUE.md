# Equipment catalogue — initial list and research ledger

Status: **Initial catalogue approved 2026-11-25; all 145 game-reference entries populated using approved representative estimates and retained verified data.** Publication and cross-system validation remain pending.

## Two layers, kept separate
1. **Researched real-world layer** (contemporary price range in USD, weight in pounds and ounces, capacities and relevant specifications). Each value needs a credible source (manufacturer or major retailer listing, government or industry statistics), a research date and, for prices, a range rather than a single figure. Not filled yet.
2. **Fictional gameplay layer** (notes, harm profile, armor, range, required skill, era availability). These are design statistics under DESIGN-NOTES 7.1, not researched facts. Era availability is a gameplay abstraction: Signal Lost and Dead Zone availability and barter values are not researched future prices (7.2, A3).

## Availability codes
C Common, R Restricted (needs a licence, credential, contact or seller), Ra Rare, U Unavailable. Order is Blackbox Protocol era / Signal Lost / Dead Zone. Defaults are per category; overrides are per item. All provisional until playtested.

## Historical research ledger (superseded by estimation policy)
Searched 2026-11-25 with the web search tool for these categories: handheld radios, portable power stations, water filters, first aid kits, tents, inverter generators, used vehicle prices and hand tools. The search returned retailer and review links but **not page text**, so **only the five entries marked with a source and 2026-11-25 date below have been verified; all other masses and prices are still TBR**. Candidate sources found, to be opened and read in the next research pass:
- Radios: REI Midland GMRS pages, manufacturer pages for Midland and Baofeng.
- Power: Honda EU2200i specification page (powerequipment.honda.com); Gear Patrol, OutdoorGearLab and Outdoor Life power station reviews.
- Water: REI and Sixmoon Designs Sawyer Squeeze pages.
- Medical: REI and Backcountry Adventure Medical Kits pages.
- Shelter: REI tent category pages.
- Vehicles: Cox Automotive, Edmunds and Kelley Blue Book used-vehicle reports.
- Tools: Home Depot product pages (hammers, drills).
Rule for the research pass: record URL, retrieval date, observed price range across at least two sellers where practical, and mass from the manufacturer's specification. If a value cannot be sourced, leave TBR.


## Historical verification note
Five entries (inverter generator, squeeze water filter, claw hammer, two-person tent, GMRS radio pair) were read from manufacturer or retailer pages on 2026-11-25. Each rests on **one source**, so the price is a single listing, not yet a researched range. Two REI pages did not match the URLs found earlier (a tent and a radio page redirected to different products), so values are for the products named in the row. Two pages were partly summary-extracted and should be rechecked. Weights are recorded in pounds and ounces; metric source values may be retained in research notes.

### Recoverable projectile durability — integrated rules draft (2026-11-25)

**Status: complete playable proposal, pending owner approval.** This resolves the prior numerical gap using the existing F17 distinction between Standard, Advanced and Simulation. The core D100 skill check remains the attack resolution; **no extra attack roll** and no mandatory independent R04 D20 circumstance roll. The condition scale below is a **proposed shared equipment-wear vocabulary**, not previously approved canon; its use for arrows and bolts is playtest-ready. Keep the equipment-condition rule consistent when later adopted for other tools.

**Projectile inventory:** EQ-101 bow uses AM-P06 arrows, six per purchased pack; EQ-102 crossbow uses AM-P07 bolts, six per purchased pack. Each projectile has a location and condition. The initial six are **serviceable**. There is no 'six shots per ammo use' conversion: Standard ammunition abstraction remains independent of the Advanced physical count.

**Proposed condition tiers (Advanced/Simulation):**

| Tier | State | Can be fired? | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Serviceable | Yes | No penalty |
| 1 | Worn | Yes | No penalty; maintenance indicated |
| 2 | Damaged | No | Repair or replacement required |
| 3 | Destroyed | No | Remove from usable stock; salvage only if fiction permits |

**Recovery sequence (Advanced):**

1. Mark the fired arrow/bolt **in field** after the normal Archery attack; do not resolve another attack or an automatic wear check.
2. After the encounter, determine whether the projectile's location is accessible. If not, mark **unrecovered** (not necessarily destroyed). A retrieval attempt, if genuinely uncertain, uses an appropriate existing D100 skill/task check and the GM's established difficulty; a visible, reachable projectile requires no roll.
3. For an accessible projectile, classify the impact: **soft** (soft ground, loose vegetation, ordinary recoverable hit), **hard** (stone, masonry, metal, severe impact) or **extreme** (fire, crushing, catastrophic environmental event). These are narrative classes, not instructions for real-world use.
4. **Proposed condition change:** soft = no change; hard = worsen one tier; extreme = worsen two tiers (maximum tier 3). A projectile recovered from an ordinary soft impact can therefore be reused indefinitely until a meaningful damaging event or loss. This avoids an arbitrary per-shot breakage lottery.
5. A Worn projectile remains usable. A Damaged projectile requires repair; a Destroyed projectile is unusable. A successful repair under the **existing F21 crafting/repair procedure** may restore one tier if materials and access permit; until a universal repair difficulty and material cost are canonized, the GM uses existing task difficulty, not a new fixed DC. Do not automatically repair a destroyed projectile.
6. Return only **recovered and usable** projectiles to the character's carried ammunition inventory. Do not count damaged, destroyed, in-field or lost items as available shots.

**Standard mode:** Keep one abstract ammunition-use accounting procedure for an encounter involving firing. A six-pack is a marketplace purchase unit, not a Standard-use conversion. Only narratively significant projectile loss or recovery needs recording; do not roll six recovery checks after every encounter.

**Simulation mode:** Record projectile-specific tier, last known location, retrieval, repair resources and salvage separately. The condition scale is the same; do not add an independent hit-point track or hidden breakage probability.

**Worked example (Advanced):** A character carries six serviceable arrows and fires three. One lands in soft ground and is accessible (tier 0, recovered); one hits stone (tier 1, recovered); the third cannot be located (unrecovered). After recovery, **five arrows are carried and usable** (three never fired + two recovered), and one is unavailable. Of the five, four are serviceable and one worn. No ammunition is created by retrieval and no extra attack roll occurs.

**Balance and scope checks:** Bow and crossbow use the **same condition rules**; bolts are not automatically sturdier or more fragile. Condition never alters the approved D8 bow/crossbow harm or the existing far-range −20. Do not grant extra Archery attacks or accuracy bonuses. Worn has no numerical penalty to avoid a double penalty from both condition and finite ammunition. 'Damaged' prevents use rather than imposing an invented −10. This proposal is compatible with F17's Advanced durability and F21's repair structure but requires explicit sign-off before its four tiers and impact changes become canonical universal equipment rules.

### Ammunition variants — cross-category combat balance pass (2026-11-25)

**Status: balanced playtest proposal; owner approval of the ammunition *selection* does not automatically approve these new mechanical effects.** Baseline attack = existing D100 Firearms roll-under (Archery for arrows/bolts); baseline damage = parent firearm's harm die. The design notes do not yet provide a finalized armor penetration ladder, cover penetration table, blast geometry or terminal-ballistics subsystem. Therefore this pass **does not invent armor-bypass numbers, universal percentage modifiers, additional damage dice or guaranteed wound effects**. Those require a later combat chapter decision. This pass finalizes a consistent **zero-power-creep default** for Core play and identifies any conditional differentiators as optional playtest-only.

**Quantitative balance invariants:** A D8 weapon remains D8 for all compatible variants (mean **4.5** raw harm); a D10 weapon remains D10 (mean **5.5** raw harm). Thus ammo selection produces **0 average raw-harm increase** and **0 unconditional attack-percentage increase** under Core, with no changes to ammo expenditure or magazine capacity. This protects the established D100 attack math and avoids creating an optimal cartridge variant solely by a free numerical bonus. The existing weapon-specific harm die always takes precedence over these examples.

**Acquisition / economy pass:** Each SKU keeps the parent's package quantity; loading a magazine transfers rounds, never creates rounds. FMJ/ordinary ball or the relevant common hunting/shotgun load is the default reference price **only where that type is actually standard for the cartridge**. Other load classes may have different prices and stock but **no numeric surcharge is canonized** without sourcing or an approved economy formula. Specialist OTM, FR, SUB, niche JHP, TR and AP may be rare, special-order, restricted or unavailable by era and faction; a label never grants supply access. Linked-ammunition feed compatibility remains mandatory. AM-C14 (.338-class) remains **unsellable until chambering is specified**. Shotgun shell length and lever-action projectile compatibility remain firearm-specific checks.

**Deferred decisions explicitly flagged for combat integration:** (1) armor material/coverage and penetration; (2) shotgun pellet/slug range behavior; (3) sound/detection and suppressor interactions; (4) optional ammunition-specific injury/evidence rules; (5) any tracer observation or suppression interaction. Do not add independent modifiers before these systems are defined, because doing so would create contradictory math. This pass is **complete as a safe Core balance baseline**, but those advanced distinctions are not yet mechanically balanced and should not be described as finalized.


### Ammunition / armor / shotgun combat reconciliation (2026-11-25)

**Status:** integrated Core rules resolution and Advanced options drafted for playtesting; no claim of owner approval for new mechanics. Cross-checked against combat-subsystems draft on `claude/project-thread-0u1ha7` (4.2 harm die, attack degree bonus, armor applied **once**, 4.3 injury severity; 7.3 Standard one ammunition use per encounter involving firing; Advanced individual loads; rapid-fire Press/Suppress rules). That draft is on a **different branch**, so its proposed rapid-fire changes must not be silently treated as merged canonical rules.

**Core attack and protection sequence (all ammunition):**

1. Confirm weapon, chambering, ammunition variant, feed compatibility, available rounds and target range. Declare the ammunition type **before** the attack.
2. Resolve the existing D100 Firearms attack, defender reaction and attack-degree bonus without additional ammunition rolls. A shotgun fires **one attack** with **one harm die**, regardless of pellet count. A slug is not a second attack.
3. On a damaging hit, roll the parent weapon's existing harm die, apply any already-established attack-degree bonus, and apply the defender's **existing armor protection exactly once**. Do not apply armor separately to pellets, bullet fragments or each point of harm. Resolve injuries using the existing severity bands (1; 2–3; 4–6; 7+) and existing instability triggers. Ammunition variants do not override injury status.
4. Spend the ammunition under the selected tracking mode. **Standard:** the existing encounter-use procedure; **Advanced:** one compatible load per ordinary shot, or the approved rapid-fire cost if rapid fire is available and actually selected. Record distinct FMJ/HP/shot/slug variants separately; never generate rounds when transferring them to a magazine.

**Shotgun payload integration:** Birdshot, buckshot and slugs are distinct purchasable shell variants. For the Core rules, **all use the shotgun's existing harm die, range bands, attack count and armor resolution**. Birdshot is not a free area attack; buckshot does not multiply damage dice; slug does not automatically penetrate armor or extend range. The different payloads can affect GM narration, physical evidence, hunting tasks and scenario-specific target suitability, but provide **no unconditional mechanical bonus**. If a scenario demands an explicit spread, multi-target, pellet-count, range or armor distinction, mark that as an **Advanced shotgun module pending separate numerical playtest**, rather than improvising contradictory modifiers.

**Armor/ammunition interaction matrix (Core):**

| Ammunition | Unarmored target | Armored target | Cover | Additional status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FMJ, HP/JHP, SP, OTM, BT, FR, SUB, lead solid/HP | Existing harm/injury rules | Existing armor once | Existing cover rules | None automatic |
| Birdshot, buckshot, slug | Existing shotgun harm/injury rules | Existing armor once | Existing cover rules | No pellet multiplication or automatic penetration |
| Tracer (restricted) | Existing harm/injury rules | Existing armor once | Existing cover rules | No automatic ignition, aiming or suppression bonus |
| Armor-piercing (restricted) | Existing harm/injury rules | Existing armor once | Existing cover rules | **No automatic bypass** until an approved armor penetration model exists |
| Muzzleloader projectile families | Existing muzzleloader harm/injury rules | Existing armor once | Existing cover rules | No automatic piercing bonus |

**Rapid-fire / suppression compatibility:** A different ammunition variant does not confer the `rapid` weapon tag. If the combat-subsystems rapid-fire proposal is adopted, Press remains one attack roll and one harm die with its specified expenditure, while Suppress uses that subsystem's existing resolution; neither gains extra dice, attack rolls, suppression strength or armor bypass from FMJ, tracer or AP labels. Keep the proposed Standard extra-use expenditure distinct from the normal Standard encounter-use cost.

**Advanced optional differentiation — strictly nonnumeric until tested:** Record shell payload, bullet construction, velocity class, armor material/coverage, obstruction and forensic evidence as scene facts. A GM may rule that a projectile is unsuitable for a declared **noncombat** task (e.g., particular hunting/game-processing objectives), but may not impose universal ballistic penalties or armor bypass without a published testable module. Avoid claiming that soft-point guarantees a kill, tracer is always visible, subsonic is silent, or AP defeats every armor type.

**Cross-system acceptance checks:** (a) No attack grants more than one harm die merely due to pellet count or fragmenting construction. (b) Armor is applied once per hit, never per pellet. (c) One load is spent per normal Advanced attack; the Standard encounter-use count is not converted from six-round packages. (d) Magazine and chambering compatibility remain mandatory. (e) Existing firearm range bands and D100 modifiers remain unchanged. (f) No ammo variant can duplicate a rapid-fire tag, cover penalty, critical effect, or injury penalty. (g) All 17 parent ammunition families remain accounted for, with AM-C14 exact chambering still unresolved.

**Deferred design requirement, not silently 'finished':** Quantified shotgun spread, armor material-specific penetration and suppressor/sound detection would require armor statistics, range definitions and probability playtests not present in the current approved rules. Core play is reconciled and playable without them; Advanced numerical differentiation remains open.
