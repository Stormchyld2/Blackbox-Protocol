# Organizations and faction templates

Status: **Approved by Jason 2026-11-25 (nine records for the three established organizations across three eras, plus four generic templates); content drafted as the working basis and provisional.** Owning book: GM Handbook, with era entries repeated by Signal Lost and Dead Zone. Records follow the F23 faction record and the C4 rules (DESIGN-NOTES 8.4): a faction's strength is read from its ledgers (members, labor hours, stock, facilities), not from a separate score. Clock targets are 4 minor, 6 significant, 8 major, 12 regional. All names are original working names. The organizations are fictional and not tied to real companies. CindrixOps is an optional name for ORG-01; no lore beyond this file is approved. ORG-04 corporate identity is approved separately below; its full faction record remains pending.

Continuity rule: each organization keeps one ID across eras. Its era record shows what the same people, assets and objectives look like then. Which scenario applies (Cascade, Quiet Spread, Insolvency) changes the details, never the identity.

## ORG-01 Regional infrastructure contractor (optional name: CindrixOps)
**Purpose.** Operates and maintains utilities, communications and control systems for local governments under multi-year contracts.
**Leadership (roles).** Regional manager (Community leader archetype, Competent), operations supervisor (Technician, Expert), contracts officer.
**Relationships.** Clients: county and municipal governments. Competes with and subcontracts to ORG-02 for site security and logistics. Cooperates with ORG-03 in emergencies.

| Era | State | Assets and ledgers | Objectives and clocks | Regional influence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blackbox Protocol | Profitable and overstretched. Deferred maintenance is hidden behind paperwork. Zero Day's antagonist organization. | Depots, vehicles, control rooms, spare-parts stock, 60–200 staff; heavy dependence on contracts. | Keep the county contract (clock 6); reduce cost (clock 4). | Strong in utilities and local politics in its region. |
| Signal Lost | Contracts unpaid or disputed in some scenarios; staff leave. The organization triages repairs. | Depots remain; spare parts and fuel are scarce; staff down to 20–60. | Restore critical sites (clock 8); protect parts stock (clock 4). | Holds the only trained crews and records, so becomes essential or resented. |
| Dead Zone | Breaks into successor groups or merges into settlement councils. Some keep manuals and tools. | Salvaged equipment and a few skilled families; labor measured in hours committed. | Keep one system running (clock 8); train replacements (clock 6). | Local: whoever holds the know-how holds leverage. |

*Scenario notes.* Cascade: the control-system faults are the origin. Quiet Spread: staffing collapses first. Insolvency: unpaid contracts drive it into crisis.
*Known/hidden.* In the Blackbox Protocol era, the fact that inspection records were falsified is hidden (see ZERO-DAY-BRIEF.md).

## ORG-02 Private security and logistics firm
**Purpose.** Provides guards, transport and warehousing for businesses, utilities and events.
**Leadership (roles).** Operations director (Law enforcement officer archetype, Expert), dispatch lead, field supervisors (Security guard archetype).
**Relationships.** Client of ORG-01 and others; uneasy with local police; hires volunteers from ORG-03 at times.

| Era | State | Assets and ledgers | Objectives and clocks | Regional influence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blackbox Protocol | Ordinary commercial firm with licensed guards and a vehicle fleet. | Vehicles, warehouses, radio network, 100–300 staff. | Win contracts (clock 6); stay within licensing (clock 4). | Moderate; known to every large local employer. |
| Signal Lost | Becomes crucial for moving and guarding scarce goods. Pressure to overstep or to refuse jobs. | Fleet short on fuel; guards stay when paid in goods. | Secure key convoys (clock 6); keep staff loyal (clock 6). | Rising; its choices decide who gets supplies. |
| Dead Zone | Splinters into militia groups, convoy guilds or armed traders; some stay honorable and some become raiders. | A few working vehicles, weapons, and trusted crews. | Hold a trade route (clock 8); keep the code (clock 4). | Regional where routes matter. |

*Scenario notes.* Insolvency: payroll failures spark defections. Cascade: fuel and radios become its main assets.

## ORG-03 Civic emergency volunteer network
**Purpose.** Community volunteers trained for disaster response, shelters, first aid and communications.
**Leadership (roles).** Coordinator (Community leader, Competent), training lead (Medic archetype), radio officer (Technician).
**Relationships.** Works with emergency managers; borrows equipment from ORG-01 and ORG-02; trusted by residents.

| Era | State | Assets and ledgers | Objectives and clocks | Regional influence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blackbox Protocol | Small, mostly unpaid, strong local trust. Holds drills and shelters. | Training hall, radios, supplies for a few days, 40–150 volunteers. | Grow membership (clock 4); win funding (clock 6). | Low in politics, high in goodwill. |
| Signal Lost | Becomes the backbone of neighbourhood response. Needs supplies and medical support. | Shelters, volunteer hours, shared stock. | Run shelters (clock 6); keep supply lines (clock 6). | Large locally; shapes how scarcity is shared. |
| Dead Zone | Often grows into a settlement's governing or aid council. | Community ledgers, clinic, school, labor registry. | Provide care (clock 6); keep order by consent (clock 8). | Settlement-wide; can rival armed groups. |

*Scenario notes.* Quiet Spread: volunteers are the first casualties and the core of care. Insolvency: becomes the main source of local authority.

## Faction templates (four)
Use the F23 record. Each template gives defaults the GM edits.

**TPL-A Local government.** Purpose: maintain order and services. Leadership: elected or appointed officials. Assets: buildings, records, taxes or reserves (era-dependent). Typical clocks: restore a service (6), pass a measure (4). Era note: authority weakens when staff and money vanish; in Dead Zone it becomes a settlement council.

**TPL-B Community cooperative.** Purpose: share production and labor. Leadership: rotating council. Assets: shared tools, fields, stock, a labor registry. Typical clocks: build a facility (8), feed the settlement through winter (6). Era note: fits Signal Lost and Dead Zone best.

**TPL-C Trade guild or merchant house.** Purpose: move goods and set trade terms. Leadership: guild master and delegates. Assets: vehicles or pack animals, warehouse, credit records. Typical clocks: open a route (6), corner a good (4). Era note: currency in Blackbox Protocol, barter and local accounting in Dead Zone (A3).

**TPL-D Armed group.** Purpose: defend, extract or control. Leadership: a commander with lieutenants. Assets: weapons, a base, a patrol area. Typical clocks: hold territory (6), take a site (8). Hostility is not mandatory: it can protect, tax or raid (F23). Era note: private security in the Blackbox Protocol era; militia or raiders later.
