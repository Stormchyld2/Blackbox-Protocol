- Unspent personal competency budgets (Elena 2, Priya 6, Samuel 2) are legal; do not automatically allocate them. Confirm intentional design before final approval.
- Samuel starts Nursing practice III and targets IV. Under the *proposed* simplified eligibility rule, he does not need 60,000 global CDR, but still needs all level-specific prerequisites, training hours, supervision, resources and assessment. Do not silently apply this proposal to an approved character sheet.
- Elena's Fire service operations III → IV is another explicit progression regression case.
- Each sheet explicitly leaves starting funds/debts, contact limits, access records, relationships and goals for player/GM completion. For ready-to-play published pregens, supply a GM-approved default or a clearly labeled fill-in field; do not invent assets or permissions.
- Each character points to a starting inventory kit, but kit prices, carry mass, quantities and any occupation-specific access restrictions must be cross-checked against STARTING-INVENTORIES.md and the equipment catalogue in the next pass.
- Combat, survival and scene utility require human playtest; numeric character-creation validity does not establish encounter balance.
