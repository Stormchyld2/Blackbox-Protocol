## Assessment
- **Mechanical consistency: PASS.** Formula is predictable, level-scaled and independent of retired CDR eligibility.
- **Campaign pacing: OPEN / MAJOR.** c6–7 advanced training may exceed plausible playable downtime in shorter campaigns; 1,280 hours for Samuel is not automatically a real-world professional credential, but still has large game-time impact.
- **Choice incentives: OPEN / MAJOR.** Low-complexity CDR/hour efficiency is 11.43× high-complexity efficiency at the extremes; slot farming may dominate for optimizing players.
- **Professional credibility: CONDITIONAL.** Qualified instruction, resource access, simulation and assessments are mandatory. No blanket hour reduction should bypass demonstrated competence or legal/fictional authorization.
- **Fairness across eras: OPEN.** BP/SL/DZ different base slot counts and resource scarcity may make nominally identical hours have very different opportunity cost.
