**Status:** analytical review completed; **no training-hour formula change approved or made**. Current canonical simplified CDR eligibility removes global CDR gates; target training hours, assessments and CDR rewards remain unchanged. This review is not a human campaign playtest.

## Reward efficiency
Incremental CDR per hour is constant across levels for a given complexity, because both double each level: c1 25 CDR/h; c2 25; c3 18.75; c4 10; c5 6.25; c6 3.75; c7 2.1875. The c1/c7 ratio is 11.43×. Extra concurrent learning slots unlock at cumulative 10,000/25,000/50,000/100,000/200,000/400,000 CDR. Removing global eligibility gates makes low-complexity accumulation a possible route to slot optimization; assessment/resource overhead may temper but is not quantified. No evidence supports changing slot thresholds or CDR awards yet.

## Assessment
- **Mechanical consistency: PASS.** Formula is predictable, level-scaled and independent of retired CDR eligibility.
- **Campaign pacing: OPEN / MAJOR.** c6–7 advanced training may exceed plausible playable downtime in shorter campaigns; 1,280 hours for Samuel is not automatically a real-world professional credential, but still has large game-time impact.
- **Choice incentives: OPEN / MAJOR.** Low-complexity CDR/hour efficiency is 11.43× high-complexity efficiency at the extremes; slot farming may dominate for optimizing players.
- **Professional credibility: CONDITIONAL.** Qualified instruction, resource access, simulation and assessments are mandatory. No blanket hour reduction should bypass demonstrated competence or legal/fictional authorization.
- **Fairness across eras: OPEN.** BP/SL/DZ different base slot counts and resource scarcity may make nominally identical hours have very different opportunity cost.

## Recommendation / next phase guardrails
**Keep current hour formula provisionally** rather than introduce untested discounts. For F36 observed sessions, compare short (1–3 months), medium (6–12 months) and long (multi-year) campaign calendars, at 1/4/8 actual training hours per available day, including pauses, resource interruptions and simultaneous projects. Capture time to first meaningful advancement, target completion, number of abandoned projects, opportunity cost and player perception. Test any later targeted reductions against all 550 records, occupation baseline grants and six pregens. Do not represent a 1,280-hour competency as mandatory real-world clinical licensing. If the high-tier time burden is unacceptable in observed play, propose a separately approved, evidence-based pacing mechanism (e.g. supervised field-practice credit with nonduplicated hours) rather than silently changing the global multiplier.
