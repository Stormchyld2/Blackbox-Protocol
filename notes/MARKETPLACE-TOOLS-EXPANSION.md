Status: proposed expansion, 2026-11-25. This is a merchandising and product-authoring plan, **not** a claim that the additional SKUs already exist. All measurements in US customary units (lb, oz, in, ft). All manufacturers and model names must be fictional. Preserve F17/F18/F20/F21/F22 rules and avoid automatic equipment bonuses.

## Shared products, without double counting
A tool may be listed by multiple stores, but its unique SKU is counted **once**. A store listing is a separate record referencing the SKU, with store-specific stock, price/discount, access and shipping. Product ownership (manufacturer) is separate from seller. Existing EQ records are equipment archetypes, and the 575 branded products are product records: do not count them again merely because they appear in a new department. Distinct sizes/materials/contents warrant distinct SKU records; alternate sellers do not.

## SKU specification checklist
For each new product: unique ID; fictional manufacturer and model; parent EQ archetype or new approved category; seller department; physical dimensions and unit mass (lb/oz); included components; power/fuel/battery dependencies; condition and consumables; plausible game-estimate USD price; BP/SL/DZ availability; stock/GM restrictions; shipping class; packaging mass; relevant skill and existing tool adequacy; no new unapproved combat modifier. Price and stock are gameplay estimates, not researched offers.

## Balancing and economic controls
- Avoid mechanical power creep: better tools can remove an existing improvised-tools penalty only where appropriate, not stack new skill bonuses.
- Preserve tool use as a prerequisite for F21 recipes/F22 facilities; ownership alone does not satisfy materials, labor or power.
- A kit's contents must be itemized or treated as one indivisible kit; do not also grant each component as free inventory.
- Heavy machinery and workshop fixtures use freight/local collection, not ordinary parcel delivery.
- Cindrix and Ironfield may overlap on basic drills or hammers; Ironfield specializes in professional variants. Harvest and Ridgeline may share axes/saws but offer distinct field and farm products.
- Before assigning new item counts, reconcile existing EQ-029–EQ-054, EQ-067, EQ-083, EQ-088, EQ-118, EQ-120–EQ-129, EQ-143 and matching MARKET-PRODUCTS SKUs to prevent duplicates.
