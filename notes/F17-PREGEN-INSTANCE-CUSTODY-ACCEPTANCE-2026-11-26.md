## Record contract
For each actual item or divisible stock lot record: unique instance/lot ID; EQ definition ID; unit and quantity; legal owner; present custodian; physical location; access basis and restriction; condition (usable/degraded/unusable); maintenance state; carried/worn/stored; contents/charge or ammunition as separate stock where appropriate; source transaction/evidence. An occupation kit's proposed EQ ID is **not** an automatic transfer from employer stores to a character.

Do not generate fictional serial numbers, assume an employer's consent, or mark a proposed item physically present before GM setup. For multi-unit bundles (EQ-001 radio **pair**), one catalogue unit comprises two handsets; record the pair as a bundle or split it into two linked handset instances without doubling bundle mass, price, or grant count. EQ-035 pliers **set** is likewise one catalogue unit. Equipment recorded at an employer site is not carried in the field.

## Acceptance fixtures — expected outcomes
1. **Grant versus access:** An employer-owned radio issued to Elena remains on the department ledger, with Elena as authorized custodian only. Returning it changes custody/location, not ownership. An unauthorized attempt to transfer it into personal property is rejected.
2. **Paired units:** One EQ-001 bundle represents two handsets. Splitting the pair for two custodians preserves two physical handsets and one bundle's total mass; it must not create two full EQ-001 catalogue units.
3. **Storage and load:** Priya's garage-stored EQ-036 does not count as carried. Moving it into her carried inventory increases carried mass by its catalogue mass (8 lb) exactly once, while ownership stays unchanged.
4. **Empty versus filled:** EQ-072's listed 6 oz is container-only. Water added to it must be recorded with a measured quantity and mass, not assumed in the dry kit; removing water reduces contents without removing the bottle.
5. **Medication boundaries:** Samuel's EQ-064 does not grant access to controlled or hospital stock. Consuming an authorized quantity reduces its lot once; duplicate transaction IDs must not reduce it twice.
6. **Consumable and ammunition accounting:** No starting kit includes a firearm, ammunition lot, or free extra batteries unless explicitly catalogued in the item bundle. Moving stock between containers or magazines does not create or consume it; firing/using a charge consumes it once per event under the applicable mode.
7. **Condition:** A degraded tool remains owned but applies the established condition rule when relevant. Unusable equipment is not available for its task. Repair requires authorized time, resources and a relevant skill, with no universal passive wear rate.
8. **Reservation and transfer:** Reserving shared stock lowers available quantity but not on-hand quantity. An atomic transfer moves custody/stock between locations without changing total stock; reject negative balances and repeated transaction IDs.
9. **Era and tier:** Constrained/ordinary/well-supported selection, rarity and era availability are checked before granting any proposed kit. No secondary-occupation duplicate kit and no automatic cross-era resupply.
10. **Scene-load acceptance:** Calculate carried ounces only after assigning each instance a scene location, adding actual contents and checking effective STR converted to pounds. Dry kit mass alone is not an encumbrance verdict.
