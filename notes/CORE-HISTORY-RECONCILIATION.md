## Confirmed agreements
1. **1962–1990:** Looking Glass origins and Blackbox Architecture precede the principal 1991 divergence. The complete public and secret histories agree.
2. **1991:** the **Blackbox Initiative** begins as a legitimate continuity effort; the later **Blackbox Protocol** designation evolves in the 2000s. The public manuscript specifies 2005–2006 for terminology, which is compatible with the approved broader 2001–2015 transition. Preserve both levels of precision.
3. **1998 / 2004 / 2016 / 2017:** Port Meridian, Meridian Accords, Harrowgate and the Fragmentation Audit have coherent public/GM counterparts. Keep restricted truths exclusively in GM-facing material.
4. **2026:** functioning institutions, morally varied actors, global campaigns, real geography and player agency. Neither manuscript makes the Blackbox network omnipotent or the collapse compulsory.
5. **Corporate powers:** Cindrix Corporation, Veydran Group and Asterion Holdings agree with the existing corporate guide. Their legitimate and conflicting activities are part of the setting, not universal villainy.

## Issues resolved as editorial boundaries, not destructive rewrites
### A. Existing regional templates versus Briar Hollow
`REGIONS-AND-SETTLEMENTS.md` already defines **R1 Calder County** (Fenwick and Alder Crossing), **R2 Aldane Coast** (Port Aldane and Kestrel Bay), and **R3 Ridgeback Highlands** (Hollis Gap). These are independent fictional sample regions, **not** the county containing Briar Hollow. Briar Hollow is an additional fictional West Virginia town whose county remains unnamed. Do not rename, erase, or silently relocate R1–R3 to make room for Briar Hollow.

### B. Existing Zero Day adventure versus Briar Hollow campaign
`ZERO-DAY-BRIEF.md` explicitly places **Dead Air** in R1's county and has its own missing technician, alert malfunction, contractor wrongdoing and evidence structure. `CORE-WORLD-AND-CAMPAIGN-FOUNDATION.md` proposes a different three-session Briar Hollow scenario. They must remain **distinct adventures**, even though both can feature infrastructure records. Do not overwrite Zero Day, transfer its fixed clues to Briar Hollow, or imply Briar Hollow is Fenwick.

### C. Contractor name collision
`ORGANIZATIONS.md` calls ORG-01 a regional infrastructure contractor with an **optional** CindrixOps label; `CORPORATE-AND-RETAIL-GUIDE.md` expressly says Cindrix Corporation's Operations division is **not established as related to ORG-01**. The histories' use of Cindrix must not convert ORG-01 into a Cindrix subsidiary or make its Zero Day wrongdoing attributable to Cindrix without a separately approved change. Prefer ORG-01 or 'regional infrastructure contractor' in cross-references.

### D. Timeline firewall
The public manuscript's Part IX includes short sections describing Signal Lost and Dead Zone as later eras. Those passages are **editorial/era framing**, not 2026 public knowledge and not new canonical future-event lore. For the Core Player's Handbook, move this framing to an out-of-world 'About the Eras' appendix or omit it from the in-world history; **preserve the original text** in the full source manuscript. The GM history may discuss uncertain warnings but must not declare the collapse inevitable or assign a fixed cause. Approved era start years: Core default 2026; Signal Lost 2031; Dead Zone 2046.

### E. Real-world names and fiction
Clarksville, Tennessee, Fort Campbell, Kentucky and other real geographic locations may be used. Do not suggest real installations, named personnel, or real corporations actually committed invented abuses. Fictionalize sensitive incidents and personnel; include the alternate-history disclaimer in published books.

### F. Continuity Zero
Keep the historical existence and original purpose of Continuity Zero GM-only. Its **present operational status** remains a GM-controlled open variable. The player history must not disclose it as a fact.

### G. Publication status and canon
The two complete-length history drafts are committed and their repository contents have been verified against the source files. Proposed named NPCs, incidents and Briar Hollow scenario elements in the campaign foundation must be cross-referenced before they are declared fixed canon. This report confirms compatibility and records specific boundaries; it does not falsely label manuscript import, full mechanical cross-validation, or adventure production as complete.
