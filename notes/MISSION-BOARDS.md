# Mission boards — 10 sample missions per era

Status: **Proposed (requested by Jason 2026-11-25); drafted as an optional GM tool, awaiting approval.** A mission board presents choices with stakes and deadlines, never forced quests (F26). Players may decline, negotiate or pursue something else, and the world moves on without them (F24). Missions use only existing rules and the records in REGIONS-AND-SETTLEMENTS.md (settlements R1–R3) and ORGANIZATIONS.md (ORG-01 contractor, ORG-02 security and logistics, ORG-03 volunteer network). **Rewards are described in kind, not as prices:** in the Blackbox Protocol era the GM sets a fee in local currency from the catalogue (A2); in Signal Lost and Dead Zone the GM sets goods, labor or favors from local conditions (A3). No fixed amounts are approved. Every mission offers at least two approaches, and no essential information is gated behind one roll (F15, R02).

Columns: **Where**, **Client**, **The job**, **Stakes and deadline**, **Approaches**, **Pay in kind**.
