# Hazards and animals

Status: **Approved by Jason 2026-11-25 (about twenty grounded hazards; representative real-world animal profiles); content drafted as the working basis, provisional until playtested.** Owning books: Core Rulebook (hazard rules) and Survival Handbook (catalogue); animals shared with Dead Zone. Supernatural creatures and unexplained phenomena are out of scope here and live only in the Anomaly Compendium (ANOMALY-CATALOGUE.md).

## Hazards
Hazards use existing rules only. Harm is an D20 base harm plus severity profile from DESIGN-NOTES 4.2 (low Low profile, moderate Moderate profile, high High profile, extreme Extreme profile), without a degree bonus for environmental harm, then armor if it applies, then injury severity under 4.3. Risk is signalled in advance by a **warning clue** (Search, PER or the named skill) so players can avoid it; a failed check costs time or resources and never removes the path (F15). Mitigation changes the difficulty, using only the R03 ladder. Stress follows 4.4 pressure events when the situation is truly frightening. This is gameplay abstraction, not safety guidance.

## Animals
Real-world animals use the same record as NPCs, with fewer entries. Attribute values are **fictional gameplay statistics, not zoological data**. Derived Health uses the Section 3.4 formula max(1, floor((STR+CON)/10)). Harm uses Section 4.2; armor is natural protection only (0–1). Animals are not monsters: most avoid people and fight only when cornered, hungry or protecting young. Livestock yields (milk, eggs, meat, labor) feed the F20 resource ledger. Care and handling use Animal Husbandry; wild encounters use Survival or Stealth.
