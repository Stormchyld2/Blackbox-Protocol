# Marketplace operations and standalone application specification

Status: initial working defaults for GM review and F18/F36 calibration; not implemented or playtested. Companion to CORPORATE-AND-RETAIL-GUIDE.md and MARKET-PRODUCTS.json. All money is fictional campaign money. No real orders, payment processing, external storefronts or real shipping.

## Catalogue
575 additional SKU records, 145 inherited archetypes, 30 fictional manufacturer identities, six storefronts. Each SKU has a distinct brand/model listing, estimated price in integer cents, unpacked weight in ounces, package description, era availability, shipping class, tax class, default stock and condition. Model tiers are commercial positioning only; no skill, harm, protection, capacity or durability bonus is implied. Current variants retain reference package and mass; individually differentiated engineering specifications are future editorial work. Display pounds and ounces. Vehicles and animals are assets, not carried inventory. Generic fictional restricted items are collection-only and require GM approval; no real purchasing links.

## Checkout defaults
All figures below are proposed fictional defaults, configurable per campaign. F18 local quotes and GM overrides prevail. Tax off by default; when enabled use a configurable destination rate, initial sample 6% general merchandise, 0% grocery and medical. These are fictional simplifications, not real tax law. Tax applies to discounted taxable merchandise only, not shipping, under default policy. Round half-up to integer cents once per tax class per seller order. No tax on barter unless GM explicitly defines one.

| Class | Standard fee | Standard delivery | Expedited |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Parcel | $5.99 plus $0.40 per lb above 5 lb | 3–5 business days | $12.99 plus $0.75 per lb above 5 lb; 1–2 days |
| Heavy parcel | $24.99 plus $0.60 per lb above 40 lb | 5–8 business days | GM quote |
| Freight | $89 plus $0.35 per lb above 70 lb | 7–14 business days | GM quote |
| Local collection | $0 | 1–3 business days to prepare | Not offered |
| Controlled collection | $0 | GM approval then 1–3 business days | Not offered |

Round billable parcel weight up to next whole pound, including packing allowance: max(4 oz, ceil(merchandise oz × 0.10)) per shipment; freight packing allowance 5% rounded up. Split by seller and shipping class; controlled and local collection lines are separate. Freight above 500 lb, vehicles, animals and unusual destinations require explicit GM quote or collection; do not calculate a misleading automatic freight fee. Freight availability is not guaranteed. Standard parcel shipping free on eligible seller subtotal of $75 or more after discounts. Cindrix Plus proposed $9.99 per 30 campaign days, free standard parcel shipping on Cindrix Market and $5.99 expedited base fee plus normal weight surcharge; no benefits at other stores. No automatic renewal unless opted in. No stacking shipping discounts. Store pickup is free where GM confirms stock and access.

## Logistics
Each retailer has a virtual national warehouse network and regional outlets. Initial stock per SKU is a scenario seed, not unlimited inventory. GM assigns region, facility, accessible stock, service coverage, working days and local lead-time modifier. No exact facility locations are canon yet. Standard delivery defaults to upper end of quoted window for deterministic simulation; GM can set exact arrival. Order states: draft, quoted, reserved, paid, processing, dispatched, delivered, collected, cancelled, failed, returned. Collection skips dispatch. Quote expires after one campaign day by default. Validate stock, funds, restrictions, destination, service and consent at confirmation. Atomically debit funds and reserve stock; use unique transaction IDs and idempotent confirmation. On dispatch move reserved stock to shipment; on delivery or collection transfer once to inventory. No inventory transfer at payment. Cancel before dispatch releases stock and refunds payment; after dispatch GM adjudicates loss or return. Default return window 30 campaign days for unused eligible merchandise; consumables opened, controlled goods and special orders require GM review. Refund actual paid merchandise and applicable tax; original shipping refunded only for seller fault by default. No real calendar dependency.

## Era behavior
BP: normal service unless scenario overrides. SL: GM enables only surviving regions, facilities, payment methods and routes; use explicit local quotes, revised stock and delivery estimates. No automatic universal inflation or guaranteed shipping. DZ: storefronts offline by default; browse archived catalogue for reference, not live orders. Local trade uses F18 accepted units and barter; no automatic USD conversion. Existing orders and currency ownership persist as records even when fulfillment or acceptance fails.

## Standalone local-browser software
Offline-first, six branded storefronts, shared authoritative catalogue and campaign ledger. No internet required. Player views: storefront home, categories, search, filters, product detail, cart, checkout, order tracking, purchase history, inventory export. GM views: campaign creation, date/era, character wallets, region and stock, restrictions, quotes, tax toggle/rates, shipping policy, orders, refunds, audit log. Use local file import/export and backups; no real accounts or payments. Single local instance initially; multiplayer synchronization and authentication architecture remain undecided. Separate product, retailer, manufacturer, stock, quote, order, shipment, wallet and transaction records. Integer cents and ounces internally; display currency and lb/oz. Preserve base EQ references and export purchased quantities with stable SKU and EQ IDs. Enforce GM-only data boundaries if network sharing is later implemented.
